# Commander's Intent

## Core scientific intent
**ENGINEER THE TERRAIN, NOT THE ANIMAL.**

**SHAPE THE FIELD != PRESCRIBE THE ORGANISM.**

The objective is not to hand-author the learner's internal representation. It is to engineer the learner-visible experience geometry so useful relations are identifiable and transferable, then let optimization determine the internal embodiment.

## Field/lattice interpretation
Training examples are not treated merely as a bag of independent pairs. The project is interested in positions, adjacency, contrast edges, neighborhoods, update-fields, and the relational structures that survive changes in surface realization.

The key operational scales now under pressure are:
`cell -> neighborhood -> sequence -> accumulation/update-field -> training trajectory`.

With microbatch 1 and gradient accumulation 8, one optimizer-visible update-field is **8 sequences x 4 cell exposures = 32 exposures** before an optimizer step.

## Research posture
A positive result is not rescued; a negative result is not explained away. The -71 bounded-transport result is treated as more scientifically valuable than the favorable +43 because it exposes the missing mechanism.

The phrase **“CFE works, we just need to dial it in” is not authorized.**

Current authorized statement:
**core observation established; mechanism unresolved.**
