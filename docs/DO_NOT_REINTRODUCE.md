# Do Not Reintroduce


## CFE program-definition regressions
- Do **not** redefine CFE as better SFT ordering, better curricula, synthetic-data coverage, optimizer scheduling, benchmark training, or efficient teaching of a curator-known rule.
- Do **not** let a successful Identifying-CFE experiment silently redefine the whole program.
- `IDENTIFYING CFE != DEVELOPMENTAL CFE`.
- `CURATOR ONTOLOGY != LEARNER ONTOLOGY`.
- `DESIGNED ENVIRONMENT != DESIGNED INTERNAL REPRESENTATION`.
- Preserve genuine UNKNOWN/ambiguity where evidence does not justify collapse.
- The governing invariant is: **The developmental structure of experience itself is the engineering medium.**

## Evidence and verifier regressions
- Do not let a verifier enforce distinctions beyond tested/earned evidence.
- Do not rewrite historical FAIL/PASS records when later evidence changes interpretation; add a new interpretation/verifier lineage instead.
- Do not promote an external prose reproduction report to hash-verified evidence without admitting/binding its artifacts.
- Do not confuse execution path with canonical artifact identity.
- Do not treat copied code/weights as a portable experiment unless the runtime/environment contract is also recovered or requalified.
