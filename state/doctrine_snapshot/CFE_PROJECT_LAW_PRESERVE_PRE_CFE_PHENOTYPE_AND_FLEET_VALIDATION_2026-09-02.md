# CFE Project Law — Preserve Pre-CFE Phenotype and Use the Fleet as a Validation Surface

Activated: 2026-09-02 08:14 Eastern Daylight Time
Status: **ACTIVE PROJECT LAW / CORE RESEARCH INTENT**
Authority: explicit operator directive following observed poor local-model autonomous-loop behavior.

## Law

> **Do not erase embarrassing pre-CFE model behavior. Preserve it as baseline phenotype evidence. If and when CFE earns intervention authority, the heterogeneous local-model fleet becomes a controlled multi-learner validation surface.**

Operationally:

`BAD BASELINE != TRASH`
`EMBARRASSING OUTPUT = POTENTIAL PRE-INTERVENTION PHENOTYPE EVIDENCE`
`CFE PROMISE != FLEET TRAINING AUTHORIZATION`
`ONE LEARNER EFFECT != CFE LAW`
`FLEET HETEROGENEITY = HOSTILE VALIDATION SURFACE`

## Required preservation
Where practical, preserve representative pre-CFE failures including:
- semantic basin capture / topic drift;
- loss of the governing question over long autonomous trajectories;
- false convergence;
- unsupported synthesis;
- brittle long-horizon continuity;
- composition failures;
- poor relevance control;
- inability to retain current constraints while integrating new evidence;
- excessive fluent continuation after evidentiary support has failed.

These are not automatically valid benchmarks. They are **candidate phenotype probes** and must later be frozen, scored, and attacked for leakage and evaluator bias.

## Fleet-validation law
When CFE reaches an intervention threshold strong enough to justify broader training, each participating learner SHALL be evaluated through at least:
1. **BASE / untouched learner**;
2. **MATCHED ORDINARY TRAINING CONTROL** — same atomic material/dose/resource envelope where scientifically feasible, without the CFE field geometry intervention;
3. **CFE DEVELOPMENTAL TRAINING** — same matched material under the prospectively defined CFE geometry.

Where possible, compare within model family and then across model families.

## Scientific target
The fleet is not merely a collection of models to improve. It is a set of different learner regimes `L` that can pressure CFE's proposed response surface:

`PHENOTYPE = F(DEVELOPMENTAL_GEOMETRY, LEARNER_REGIME, DOSE, OPTIMIZER, HISTORY, TASK_CLOSURE, ...)`

The aim is to distinguish:
- learner-specific sensitivity;
- model-family-specific sign flips;
- optimizer/runtime mediation;
- capacity effects;
- integration-horizon differences;
- geometry effects that survive learner changes;
- coordinate-system failures exposed only by heterogeneous learners.

## Claim discipline
Fleet improvement is not itself proof of CFE.
Fleet consistency is not itself proof of universality.
Fleet disagreement is not noise by default.

`CROSS-LEARNER SIGN FLIP -> REGIME/AXIS AUDIT`
`CROSS-LEARNER CONSISTENCY -> REPLICATION SIGNAL, NOT UNIVERSAL LAW`

## Authorization gate
No broad local-fleet retraining is authorized merely by this law. Fleet training requires a later frozen plan that specifies:
- CFE intervention being tested;
- matched-control construction;
- learner inventory and eligibility;
- pre-CFE phenotype probes;
- compute/resource bounds;
- evaluation and adverse-outcome criteria;
- model-specific adapters/checkpoints and rollback;
- cartography binding;
- claim ceiling.
