## Program invariant

> **The developmental structure of experience itself is the engineering medium.**

CFE studies and engineers:

`developmental experience -> learner-visible relational structure -> learning pressure -> emergent cognitive phenotype`

CFE is not defined by benchmark improvement, curriculum tuning, SFT ordering, prompt engineering, optimizer scheduling, or efficient acquisition of curator-named rules.

Those can be experimental mechanisms inside CFE.

They are not the root object.

## Commander's Intent

> **Engineer the terrain, not the animal.**

CFE aims to design developmental conditions under which useful distinctions, representations, strategies, abstractions, uncertainty handling, compositions, and cognitive operating habits can emerge.

The curator may know some useful relations, but the learner's internal ontology is not assumed to match the curator's decomposition.

Therefore:

- `CURATOR ONTOLOGY != LEARNER ONTOLOGY`
- `CURATOR DECOMPOSITION != NECESSARILY LEARNER DECOMPOSITION`
- `DESIGNED ENVIRONMENT != DESIGNED INTERNAL REPRESENTATION`
- `DEVELOPMENTAL PRESSURE != COGNITIVE PRESCRIPTION`
- `BENCHMARK IMPROVEMENT != PROGRAM SUCCESS`
- `IDENTIFYING CFE != WHOLE CFE`
- `LOCAL EXPERIMENTAL FAILURE CHANGES MECHANISM, NOT COMMANDER'S INTENT`

`CURATOR DECOMPOSITION != NECESSARILY LEARNER DECOMPOSITION` is currently a live candidate law motivated by v1.2; it is not yet promoted as a universal CFE law.

## Program modes

### Identifying CFE

Known target relation or mechanism -> controlled learner-visible experience -> acquisition / transfer / mechanism test.

Purpose:

- falsifiable construction laws;
- rival-rule elimination;
- transport analysis;
- optimizer-visible geometry analysis;
- mechanism discrimination;
- hard evidence before broader developmental claims.

The v1.1, v1.2, and v1.3 bounded-transport branches are Identifying-CFE specializations.

### Developmental CFE

Known lawful environment / consequences -> engineered developmental ecology -> learner-discovered representations, distinctions, strategies, compositions, and operating phenotype.

The developmental ecology may contain:

- incomplete evidence;
- delayed consequences;
- stale knowledge;
- uncertainty;
- changing currentness;
- history;
- false analogues;
- recovery;
- resource constraints;
- multiple lawful strategies;
- genuine UNKNOWN states;
- novel compositions;
- consequences whose relevance becomes visible only later.

This remains the broader program target.

## What the field must preserve

CFE must not force every experience into a crisp answer.

When evidence is insufficient, the field should preserve uncertainty rather than reward unjustified collapse.

> Engineer experience so consequential distinctions become identifiable when sufficient evidence exists, while preserving uncertainty and ambiguity where reality does not justify collapse.

Learner-visible developmental structure may include:

- relative placement;
- contrast;
- sequence;
- recurrence;
- variation;
- dependency;
- history;
- consequence;
- absence;
- interruption;
- revision;
- delay;
- opportunities to act, probe, wait, or recover.

`LONG CONTEXT != LONG-HORIZON COGNITION`

Earlier experience must remain consequential later for history to function as developmental structure.

## Current earned empirical state

The current fixed CapybaraHermes/Mistral learner regime has earned these bounded statements:

1. **v1.0** — learner-visible experience arrangement can produce reproducible, relation-dependent behavioral effects, but the original neighborhood effect was family-dependent and not a stable overall advantage.

2. **v1.1** — broader identifying support materially changed predicate transfer relative to the under-resolved `{0,+1}` slice. Identifying-minus-narrow mean predicate delta was +17.56 points, with 5/6 positive seeds and +24.17 points outside original support. But the learned strict boundary remained unstable and one-sided.

3. **v1.2** — broader identifying predicate support did not improve zero-shot composition with a separately learned opaque policy under mixed 4+4 primitive windows. Mean composed delta was -0.69 points; identifying won 2/6 seeds. Policy learning was strong. Predicate behavior remained unstable. The strongest direct-predicate seed composed worse than its narrow mate.

Therefore the current empirical ceiling is:

> **Learner-visible experience geometry and optimizer-visible transport materially affect learned phenotype in this fixed learner regime, but CFE does not yet possess a stable predictive construction law for two-sided predicate acquisition or primitive composition.**

Do not compress this into `CFE is proven`, `CFE failed`, or `CFE is just machine teaching`.
