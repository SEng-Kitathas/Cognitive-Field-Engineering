## Program invariant

> **The developmental structure of experience itself is the engineering medium.**

CFE studies and engineers:

`developmental experience -> learner-visible relational structure -> learning pressure -> emergent cognitive phenotype`

CFE is not defined by benchmark improvement, curriculum tuning, SFT ordering, prompt engineering, optimizer scheduling, or efficient acquisition of curator-named rules.

Those can be experimental mechanisms inside CFE.

They are not the root object.

## Commander's Intent

> **Engineer the terrain, not the animal.**

CFE aims to design developmental conditions under which useful distinctions, representations, strategies, abstractions, uncertainty handling, compositions, and cognitive operating habits can emerge.

The curator may know some useful relations, but the learner's internal ontology is not assumed to match the curator's decomposition.

Therefore:

- `CURATOR ONTOLOGY != LEARNER ONTOLOGY`
- `CURATOR DECOMPOSITION != NECESSARILY LEARNER DECOMPOSITION`
- `DESIGNED ENVIRONMENT != DESIGNED INTERNAL REPRESENTATION`
- `DEVELOPMENTAL PRESSURE != COGNITIVE PRESCRIPTION`
- `BENCHMARK IMPROVEMENT != PROGRAM SUCCESS`
- `IDENTIFYING CFE != WHOLE CFE`
- `LOCAL EXPERIMENTAL FAILURE CHANGES MECHANISM, NOT COMMANDER'S INTENT`

`CURATOR DECOMPOSITION != NECESSARILY LEARNER DECOMPOSITION` is currently a live candidate law motivated by v1.2; it is not yet promoted as a universal CFE law.

### Identifying CFE

Known target relation or mechanism -> controlled learner-visible experience -> acquisition / transfer / mechanism test.

Purpose:

- falsifiable construction laws;
- rival-rule elimination;
- transport analysis;
- optimizer-visible geometry analysis;
- mechanism discrimination;
- hard evidence before broader developmental claims.

### Developmental CFE

Known lawful environment / consequences -> engineered developmental ecology -> learner-discovered representations, distinctions, strategies, compositions, and operating phenotype.

The developmental ecology may contain:

- incomplete evidence;
- delayed consequences;
- stale knowledge;
- uncertainty;
- changing currentness;
- history;
- false analogues;
- recovery;
- resource constraints;
- multiple lawful strategies;
- genuine UNKNOWN states;
- novel compositions;
- consequences whose relevance becomes visible only later.

This remains the broader program target.
