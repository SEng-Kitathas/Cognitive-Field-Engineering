# STANDARD UPLIFT — THREE-LINEAGE + TRAINING-BODY-PURITY DOCTRINE

Status: **BINDING PROJECT DOCTRINE — STANDARD DATASET DESIGN**
Date: 2026-09-02 21:23 Eastern Daylight Time

## Core object
Standard Uplift is a **modern competence dataset whose learner-visible experiences are selected and constructed using three independent informing surfaces**:

1. **Historical AI lineage (old AI research → now)** — use recurring mechanisms, failure boundaries, and state-transition structures recovered from the reasoning archaeology program.
2. **CFE-informed developmental design** — use CFE's earned constraints and research priors to shape experience structure where appropriate, without turning Standard into a CFE experimental pack.
3. **Modern AI practice/research** — preserve present-day instruction following, code, math/science, agent/tool use, web research, long-horizon/currentness, multi-turn work, and modern reasoning/revision competence.

`STANDARD UPLIFT DATASET != CFE EXPERIMENTAL PACK` remains binding.

## No canonical row/token quota
There is **no sacred row count, line count, or atom floor**. Prior A0 numeric ranges are resource-planning heuristics and historical gates, not doctrine.

The dataset is large enough when additional rows primarily add redundancy/noise rather than missing competence, mechanism coverage, boundary cases, or useful cross-domain recurrence. It is too small when known load-bearing competence/mechanism holes remain.

A token budget may still be used for hardware/run planning. It SHALL NOT justify adding weak rows or deleting high-value complete episodes merely to hit a number.

## Training body purity — hard separation
`TRAINING BODY != CONTROL PLANE`.

### Learner-visible body MAY contain only
- task-relevant user/system context required to perform the task;
- assistant responses/actions/tool calls;
- observations/results required by the episode;
- natural domain constraints and real-world policies when they are the **subject or operating rules of the task itself**;
- evidence and consequences needed for reasoning/currentness/history.

### Learner-visible body SHALL NOT contain project/dataset control-plane material
- CFE/PCMMAD doctrine or Commander intent;
- dataset manifests, hashes, provenance ledgers, row IDs, source IDs, admission states;
- contamination/eval-registry bookkeeping;
- curator labels/invariant names/coverage scores;
- quality-review notes, promotion/demotion state, trace/revisit bookkeeping;
- Git/job/runtime/resource-ownership bookkeeping;
- instructions whose only purpose is governing the dataset/training project rather than solving the represented task.

All of that lives in **sidecars, manifests, registries, audit artifacts, and selectors outside the training payload**.

### Important anti-overfilter rule
A legitimate task about corporate governance, public policy, audit, compliance, law, provenance, software manifests, or security SHALL NOT be rejected merely because those words appear. The distinction is functional: **task-world content is allowed; Standard/CFE control-plane content is not**.

## What "historically informed" means
Do **not** train the learner on historical system names or papers merely to claim heritage. Extract recurring functional pressures and embody them in ordinary modern tasks where the consequence structure matches. Candidate mechanism families include:
- state-difference/operator/subgoal control;
- live alternatives / qualified noncommitment;
- support lineage and dependency-local rollback;
- minimal revision / preservation of unaffected state;
- multiple-hypothesis temporal requalification;
- conflict/nogood reuse;
- discriminator/measurement selection;
- external evidence authority over internal preference;
- representation adequacy/refinement;
- metareasoning / value-of-computation / adaptive effort;
- learning/compression from successful or failed deliberation;
- explicit UNKNOWN/partial-model honesty.

Historical recurrence is an **informing quarry**, not learner ontology and not proof of shared implementation.

## What "CFE informed" means
Use CFE to pressure episode geometry and selection where justified:
- consequential history / LHIT where prior state matters;
- currentness and later evidence requalifying earlier state;
- identifying evidence rather than generic support;
- field-resolution / local contrast sufficiency;
- cross-neighborhood/contextual diversity where needed;
- relation-family dependence and sign/path sensitivity;
- selective preservation under revision;
- representation refinement when the current coordinate system aliases consequence-distinct states;
- curator ontology hidden from the learner.

Do not claim Standard rows are causal CFE treatments unless separately tested under CFE experimental controls.

## What "modern informed" means
The final body should remain recognizably useful for present-day models and workflows: instruction following, conversation, code/debugging, math/science, structured outputs, tools/agents, research/search, evidence synthesis, long-context/currentness, planning, diagnosis, revision, and modern multimodal/tool conventions where lawful data exists. Modernity is a competence surface, not a recency quota.

## Sufficiency / freeze rule
Freeze is earned when hostile review finds no important **uncovered competence or mechanism hole** that can be added without disproportionate contamination, redundancy, brittleness, or control-plane leakage.

Before freeze, require:
- explicit three-lineage crosswalk;
- cross-domain isomorph + anti-isomorph review for high-value mechanisms;
- modern competence/source diversity review;
- CFE-informed hidden-structure review;
- historical-mechanism recurrence review;
- contamination/dedup/license/quality gates;
- target-render integrity with no destructive history cropping;
- training-body purity audit.

No fixed N can substitute for those checks.
