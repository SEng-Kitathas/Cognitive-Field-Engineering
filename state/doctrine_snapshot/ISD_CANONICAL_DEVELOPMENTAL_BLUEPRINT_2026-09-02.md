## 0. Authority / reconciliation
This document integrates the full ISD lineage around the operator-selected conceptual center. It does **not** erase earlier artifacts. Where earlier ISD planning said the school-like partition was not frozen, that statement is superseded for **ISD engineering** by the operator's later explicit decision to keep the five-block developmental shape. The stronger causal claim remains unearned CFE science.

`FIVE-TIER ISD SHAPE = BINDING ENGINEERING`
`DEPENDENCY-ORDER CAUSAL SUPERIORITY = EXPERIMENTAL HYPOTHESIS`

## 1. Governing rule
> **Figure out what must logically become available before what, then engineer experience so those dependencies become discoverable in the right causal order.**

The unit of organization is **developmental dependency depth**, not surface difficulty, prestige, age, benchmark category, or trace length.

`DEPENDENCY DEPTH != TASK DIFFICULTY`
`STAGING != EASY-TO-HARD SORT`
`SAME FINAL INFORMATION != SAME DEVELOPMENTAL FIELD`
`SAME ATOMS + DIFFERENT DEVELOPMENTAL ORDER MAY != SAME LEARNED REPRESENTATION`

## 2. Canonical five-block developmental shape

### T0 — Foundations / K–5 alias
Build reusable primitives and direct consequence structure.

Pressures include:
- identity/difference, ordering, quantity, direct cause/effect;
- exact instruction following / transformation;
- evidence versus unsupported assertion;
- direct newer-over-stale currentness under clear authority;
- one-step action/tool -> observation/consequence;
- clean contrasts + clean anti-isomorphs;
- basic composition from already available primitives;
- honest UNKNOWN when evidence is absent.

### T1 — Relational / 6–8 alias
Make primitives interact through small relational/dependency structures.

Pressures include:
- short dependency chains;
- selective local repair and preservation of unaffected state;
- small live alternative sets;
- identifying checks rather than merely supportive evidence;
- multi-step composition;
- cross-domain isomorph + anti-isomorph transfer;
- revisit after state change;
- early support lineage.

### T2 — Systems / 9–12 alias
Reason over larger interacting systems where local correctness is insufficient.

Pressures include:
- multi-hop planning/tool use;
- dependency graphs and scoped rollback;
- competing explanations + discriminator selection;
- long-horizon/currentness through several events;
- multi-source synthesis;
- reusable conflict/failure boundaries;
- composition/transfer under changed surface form;
- representation pressure when local patches stop working.

### T3 — Open-world / College alias
Operate under incomplete information, conflicting authority, long horizons, and costly inquiry.

Pressures include:
- multi-hypothesis reason maintenance;
- source authority/currentness in research/search;
- adaptive reasoning/tool effort;
- conflicting evidence channels;
- sparse long-range consequential dependencies;
- representation refinement with conservation of still-valid state;
- STOP/CONTINUE under value-of-check vs cost;
- uncertainty preserved until identifying evidence arrives;
- state compression only when future discriminators survive.

### T4 — Frontier synthesis / target capability
Aim at the model we actually want, not a harder benchmark bucket.

Pressures include:
- novel problem decomposition;
- self-directed discriminator/measurement selection;
- transfer into unfamiliar domains;
- representation creation/replacement when current coordinates alias consequence-distinct states;
- long-horizon support/currentness maintenance;
- qualified compression + recoverability;
- efficient recruitment of cognition rather than maximal reasoning;
- recovery when the framing/model itself is wrong;
- learning from successful and failed deliberation without merely copying inner monologue.

## 3. Spiral accretion is mandatory
Stages are not sealed courses. Earlier structures recur under changed relational/consequence conditions.

Canonical progression shape:

`A`
`-> A + B`
`-> A under conflict`
`-> A after stale evidence`
`-> A across domains`
`-> A under costly inquiry`
`-> A inside representation refinement/replacement`

This is **accretion with requalification**, not duplicate padding.

Earlier primitives should become increasingly embedded, compressed, generalized and revisable—not vanish after one block and not remain permanent shackles when a higher-order representation subsumes them.

## 4. Everything learned before and after the five-block insight applies inside the shape

### 4.1 Three-lineage informing
Each stage is curated from three independent informing surfaces:
1. historical AI / reasoning archaeology (old AI -> current systems);
2. CFE developmental constraints / earned science + carefully labeled research hypotheses;
3. modern AI/post-training/tool/research competence.

Historical system names and CFE labels are not learner targets. We steal **functional invariants and consequence structures**, not vocabulary.

### 4.2 Isomorphic Predator
For high-value mechanisms:
`DONOR MECHANISM -> FUNCTIONAL CONTRACT -> POSITIVE ISOMORPH -> ANTI-ISOMORPH -> LEARNER-VISIBLE CONSEQUENCE VERIFY -> ADMIT`

Predator connects historical AI, CFE, operator/personal reasoning archaeology, modern research, and clean task families.

`SURFACE MATCH != STRUCTURAL ISOMORPH`
`POSITIVE WITHOUT ANTI-ISOMORPH != LOAD-BEARING COVERAGE`

### 4.3 Field resolution / identifying evidence
DD1 supports the narrower law that local learner-visible field resolution matters causally in the tested regime. ISD therefore prefers experiences where the distinctions needed to identify a relation become jointly available when appropriate.

But:
`IDENTIFYING CFE != WHOLE CFE`
`LOCAL COVISIBILITY != STABLE COMPETENCE SUFFICIENCY`

### 4.4 LHIT / consequential history
History matters only when earlier state remains consequential later.

`LONG CONTEXT != LONG-HORIZON COGNITION`
`LONGER HISTORY != MORE CONSEQUENTIAL HISTORY`
`TEMPORAL ORDER != CAUSAL DEPENDENCY`

### 4.5 Reason maintenance
Across stages, progressively teach environments that make useful operations necessary:
- live consequence-distinct alternatives;
- support/dependency lineage;
- local invalidation / rollback to cause rather than clock;
- conflict/nogood reuse;
- currentness propagation;
- authority transfer;
- representation adequacy/refinement;
- qualified compression;
- value-guided reasoning effort.

### 4.6 Representation adequacy
If the current coordinate system collapses states that have different relevant consequences, the proper move may be representation refinement rather than deeper search inside a bad frame.

`R(s1)=R(s2)` while `consequence(s1)!=consequence(s2)` -> missing-distinction audit -> refine/replace coordinates -> re-derive.

### 4.7 Adaptive effort
More reasoning is not the objective.

`EFFORT ~ EXPECTED DISCRIMINATORY / DECISION VALUE`

The first Predator-qualified ISD family already instantiates matched STOP/CONTINUE anti-isomorphs where the correct action reverses with evidence sufficiency and cost.

### 4.8 Learning from deliberation without training on governance or gratuitous inner monologue
Modern research and the project's own rendering work support treating reasoning information and literal teacher narration as separable training objects. ISD should preserve useful task-world state transitions and consequences, not copy private/control-plane text merely because it exists.

## 5. Stage-neutral competence support
Not every useful row must be assigned a developmental mechanism tier.

Rows whose developmental prerequisite structure is genuinely unresolved may enter as **stage-neutral support** after a conservative minimum developmental point:
- general instruction/chat support: after T1 spine;
- technical math/code/science support: after T2 spine;
- residual open research/agent support: after T3 spine.

These rows provide competence breadth but earn **no developmental-mechanism coverage credit**.

`STAGE-NEUTRAL SUPPORT != DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE`
`HARD MATH != HIGH DEVELOPMENTAL TIER`

## 6. Training-body purity is absolute
The learner sees only task-world experience:
- prompts/context required by the task;
- assistant responses/actions/tool calls;
- observations/results;
- natural domain constraints;
- consequences/history required to solve the task.

Learner-visible ISD SHALL NOT contain:
- CFE/PCMMAD doctrine;
- tier names or school aliases;
- prerequisite graph/node labels;
- Isomorphic Predator labels/pair IDs;
- personal/historical donor provenance;
- curator dimensions/tags;
- manifests, hashes, row IDs, admission state;
- contamination/eval-registry bookkeeping;
- Git/job/runtime/resource governance;
- project-specific control-plane instructions.

`TRAINING BODY != CONTROL PLANE`
`CURATOR ONTOLOGY != LEARNER ONTOLOGY`

## 7. No canonical size / quota
No sacred row count, line count, token quota, or stage percentage defines completion.

Freeze is earned when hostile review finds no important missing competence/mechanism/boundary/isomorph/anti-isomorph that is worth adding under contamination, redundancy, resource, and quality constraints.

## 8. Resource constraints must not become hidden scientific treatments
If hardware cannot safely train a complete episode at the frozen context ceiling, that episode goes to a **long-context reserve** shared by all causal arms. It is not silently cropped in one arm, and hardware limits do not redefine the conceptual tier.

`LONG-CONTEXT RESERVE != REJECTION`
`RESOURCE EXCLUSION MUST BE ARM-SYMMETRIC`
`NO DESTRUCTIVE HISTORY CROP TO FIT GPU`

## 9. ISD completion and the accretion hypothesis are separate questions
A completed ISD must first be defensible as a high-quality informed standard dataset.

The causal research question is then:
> Does dependency-respecting developmental accretion produce a different/better phenotype than an order-destroyed exposure to the same trainable information?

A serious first experiment therefore requires matched arms:

### ARM A — DEVELOPMENTAL_SPIRAL
Same frozen atoms, ordered by dependency depth + spiral requalification + conservative support entry.

### ARM B — ORDER_DESTROYED_CONTROL
Same exact atoms, same token multiset/dose, same learner, same LoRA/optimizer/eval, same initial trainable weights, but order deliberately destroys dependency-stage structure while preserving all other feasible controls.

### Primary causal variable
`DEVELOPMENTAL ORDER / PREREQUISITE TOPOLOGY`

### Required readouts
- ordinary competence / catastrophic regression;
- composition;
- cross-domain transfer;
- currentness;
- dependency-local repair / rollback selectivity;
- live-hypothesis maintenance;
- discriminator selection;
- adaptive effort;
- representation refinement/framing recovery where measurable;
- relearning/correction cost;
- intermediate-checkpoint developmental trajectories where instrumentation is available.

A gain in one arm is not attributed to accretion unless the arm identity and equality constraints survive hostile readback.

## 10. Internal-development instrumentation
Deep Research Wave 1 adds a valuable instrument path: checkpoint-level representation analysis may help observe feature emergence/consolidation/degeneration under different developmental orders.

Guard:
`FEATURE COORDINATE != LEARNER ONTOLOGY`
`REPRESENTATION PATH DIFFERENCE != COMPETENCE DIFFERENCE`
`CAUSAL EFFECT != COMPLETE SUPPORT GRAPH`

Behavior remains sovereign for competence claims; interpretability is a triangulation surface.

## 11. Completion standard
ISD is complete enough to freeze when:
- the five-tier developmental spine is explicit and internally coherent;
- prerequisite edges are justified enough for engineering use and uncertainty is labeled;
- spiral bridges/requalifications are real consequence-bearing experiences, not padding;
- stage-neutral support is placed conservatively;
- high-value mechanisms have cross-domain positive + anti-isomorph coverage or explicit accepted holes;
- modern competence breadth is preserved;
- historical/CFE informing is embodied rather than named;
- purity, license, dedup, public/private contamination, quality and render gates pass;
- no hardware workaround changes one causal arm differently from the other;
- frozen phenotype probes exist before training;
- matched-arm preregistration is sealed before outcome observation;
- explicit training authorization is issued only after all of the above.

## 12. Scientific truth boundary
### Binding engineering doctrine
- five-tier developmental dependency shape;
- spiral accretion/requalification;
- three-lineage informing;
- Isomorphic Predator/anti-isomorph curation;
- training-body purity;
- no fixed quota;
- conservative stage-neutral support;
- matched causal first-run design.

### Earned CFE science already relevant
- DD1 field-resolution effect in its fixed regime;

### Still hypothesized
- prerequisite-respecting accretion causes superior representation basins/transfer/revision;
- the specific five-tier dependency decomposition is uniquely or optimally correct;
- internal representation trajectories will cleanly mirror curator-side prerequisite structure.

The experiment is designed to find out, not to confirm the story.
