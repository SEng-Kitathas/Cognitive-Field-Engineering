# ISD — Developmental Dependency Staging Doctrine

Status: **BINDING ISD CONSTRUCTION DOCTRINE / NOT YET A CFE SCIENTIFIC LAW**
Date: 2026-09-02 22:19 Eastern Daylight Time

## Core construction law
> **Figure out what must logically become available before what, then engineer experience so those dependencies become discoverable in the right causal order.**

ISD SHALL preserve this shape during construction. The school labels are curator-side aliases for **dependency depth**, not literal age, grade, difficulty, or learner-facing metadata.

`DEPENDENCY DEPTH != TASK DIFFICULTY`
`STAGING != EASY-TO-HARD SORT`
`SAME FINAL INFORMATION != SAME DEVELOPMENTAL FIELD`

## Five developmental blocks

### T0 — Foundations / “K–5”
Goal: establish primitives and direct consequence structure that later reasoning can reuse.

Typical learner-visible pressures:
- identity/difference, order, quantity, direct cause/effect;
- instruction following and exact transformation;
- evidence vs unsupported assertion;
- simple currentness: newer fact replaces stale fact when authority is clear;
- one-step tool/action -> consequence;
- binary contrasts and clean anti-isomorphs;
- basic composition from already available primitives;
- explicit UNKNOWN when evidence is absent.

### T1 — Relational / “6–8”
Goal: make primitives interact through small dependency structures.

Typical pressures:
- small dependency chains and selective repair;
- two/few live alternatives;
- identifying checks vs merely supportive checks;
- multi-step composition;
- cross-domain isomorph/anti-isomorph transfer;
- simple revisit after state change;
- preserve unaffected state after a local correction.

### T2 — Systems / “9–12”
Goal: reason over larger interacting structures where local correctness is not enough.

Typical pressures:
- multi-hop planning/tool use;
- dependency graphs and scoped rollback;
- competing explanations with discriminator selection;
- long-horizon/currentness across several events;
- reusable failure boundaries;
- composition/transfer under changed surface form;
- representation pressure when local patches stop working.

### T3 — Open-world / “College”
Goal: operate under incomplete information, conflicting authority, long horizons, and costly inquiry.

Typical pressures:
- multi-hypothesis maintenance;
- research/search with source authority/currentness;
- adaptive reasoning/tool effort;
- conflict between evidence channels;
- sparse long-range dependencies;
- representation refinement with conservation of still-valid state;
- strategic STOP/CONTINUE under cost;
- uncertainty preserved until discriminating evidence arrives.

### T4 — Frontier synthesis / “Post-college / target capability”
Goal: produce the kind of model we actually want: capable of constructing and revising its own problem representation while preserving consequence-grounded state.

Typical pressures:
- novel problem decomposition;
- self-directed discriminator selection;
- transfer into unfamiliar domains;
- representation creation/replacement when existing coordinates alias consequential states;
- long-horizon reason maintenance;
- selective compression of solved structure;
- efficient cognition recruitment rather than maximal reasoning;
- recovery when the framing/problem model itself is wrong.

## Spiral law
Stages SHALL NOT be sealed curricula. Earlier primitives recur in later stages under harder relational contexts.

Example spiral:
`A` -> `A with B` -> `A under conflict` -> `A after stale evidence` -> `A across domains` -> `A inside a representation shift`.

The goal is **accretion with requalification**, not one-time exposure followed by abandonment.

## CFE/ISD machinery applied inside every stage
The following remain curator-side design tools and are applied at stage-appropriate depth:
- Isomorphic Predator and anti-isomorph hunting;
- identifying contrast / field resolution;
- LHIT / consequential-history recurrence;
- currentness and selective revision;
- live alternatives and discriminator selection;
- dependency-local repair;
- failure-boundary reuse;
- representation adequacy/refinement;
- adaptive reasoning effort / value of computation;
- cross-domain transfer;
- modern competence/source pressure;
- historical AI reason-maintenance invariants;
- CFE hidden developmental geometry.

## Training-body purity
Tier names, prerequisite graphs, donor lineage, CFE labels, personal provenance, curator dimensions, governance and bookkeeping SHALL remain sidecar-only.

The learner sees ordinary task-world episodes and their consequences.

`STAGE LABEL -> SIDECAR ONLY`
`PREREQUISITE GRAPH -> SIDECAR ONLY`
`DONOR PROVENANCE -> SIDECAR ONLY`

## Promotion between stages
A later-stage episode is valid only when its required primitives/relations are either:
1. established in earlier stages, or
2. intentionally introduced inside the same stage through an identifying field that makes the dependency discoverable.

Do not assume a prerequisite because it feels educationally conventional. Dependency claims must be justified by task consequence, identifiability, or explicit synthetic construction.

## No fixed stage sizes
There is no canonical row count, token quota, or percentage for any stage. Stage size follows coverage and useful recurrence.

## Scientific restraint
This is binding **ISD engineering doctrine** because it is a coherent, low-regret construction shape and preserves the user's requested developmental organization. It is not promoted as an earned universal CFE law until dependency-order experiments establish causal effects.
