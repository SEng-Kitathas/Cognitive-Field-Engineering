## Program invariant

> **The developmental structure of experience itself is the engineering medium.**

CFE studies and engineers the relationship:

`developmental experience -> learner-visible relational structure -> learning pressure -> emergent cognitive phenotype`

The project does **not** define success as merely improving benchmark accuracy, SFT ordering, curriculum quality, optimizer scheduling, or acquisition of a curator-named rule.

## Commander's Intent

CFE aims to engineer developmental terrain rather than directly prescribe the learner's internal mind.

The learner may develop representations, distinctions, strategies, abstractions, and compositions that the curator did not explicitly name.

Therefore:

- `CURATOR ONTOLOGY != LEARNER ONTOLOGY`
- `DESIGNED ENVIRONMENT != DESIGNED INTERNAL REPRESENTATION`
- `DEVELOPMENTAL PRESSURE != COGNITIVE PRESCRIPTION`
- `BENCHMARK IMPROVEMENT != PROGRAM SUCCESS`
- `IDENTIFYING CFE != WHOLE CFE`

## Two program modes

### IDENTIFYING CFE

The curator knows a target relation and engineers learner-visible experience that should make that relation identifiable.

Purpose:
- controlled mechanism science;
- falsifiable construction laws;
- rival-rule elimination;
- transfer testing;
- transport/optimizer analysis.

This is the current bounded-transport predicate/policy branch.

It is a **scientific specialization of CFE**, not the definition of CFE.

### DEVELOPMENTAL CFE

The curator engineers lawful environmental structure and consequences while allowing the learner to discover useful representations, abstractions, strategies, compositions, and operating habits that are not fully prescribed in advance.

The developmental environment may contain:
- incomplete evidence;
- delayed consequences;
- changing currentness;
- stale knowledge;
- ambiguity;
- false analogues;
- recovery;
- intervention opportunities;
- resource constraints;
- multiple lawful strategies;
- genuine UNKNOWN states;
- novel compositions.

This mode is closer to the long-range program intent.

## What the field must preserve

CFE must not force every experience into a crisp answer.

When evidence is insufficient, the field should preserve uncertainty rather than reward unjustified collapse.

The deeper rule is:

> Engineer experience so consequential distinctions become identifiable when sufficient evidence exists, while preserving uncertainty and ambiguity where reality does not justify collapse.

## Experience is more than content

Learner-visible developmental structure may include:
- relative placement;
- contrast;
- sequence;
- recurrence;
- variation;
- dependency;
- history;
- consequence;
- absence;
- interruption;
- revision;
- delayed effects;
- opportunities to act or wait.

Long context alone is not the target.

`LONG CONTEXT != LONG-HORIZON COGNITION`

Earlier experience must remain consequential later for history to function as developmental structure.

## Structural transfer

CFE values relations over surface labels.

The field should eventually support both:
- isomorphs: different surfaces sharing a useful invariant;
- anti-isomorphs: apparent transfer that breaks at an important boundary.

The learner should increasingly discover not only similarity, but where structural equivalence stops.

## Composition

The long-range goal includes generative cognition rather than a catalog of stored solutions.

If useful primitives A and B have been acquired, a mature developmental system should create conditions under which `A o B` can become discoverable even when the exact composition was not supplied as a canned behavior.

Transfer, recombination, abstraction, strategy formation, recruitment, and adaptation remain program-level targets.

## Cognitive operating phenotype

CFE may eventually shape conditions under which useful cognitive habits repeatedly prove useful, including:
- maintaining competing hypotheses;
- seeking discriminating evidence;
- preserving uncertainty;
- revising under counterevidence;
- preserving constraints;
- isolating failure;
- reasoning from mechanism;
- probing novelty and boundaries;
- reasoning over delayed consequences;
- preserving recoverability;
- switching strategies when context changes.

These are not personas to install. They are possible developmental products.

## Current empirical ceiling

Current evidence supports only the narrower statement:

> Learner-visible experience arrangement has produced reproducible, relation-dependent behavioral effects in the current fixed-learner regime; whether those effects generalize into a broader CFE law remains unresolved.

Do not compress this into `CFE is proven` or `CFE works`.

## Current scientific specialization

The active predicate/policy screen is an Identifying CFE experiment.

Its job is to test whether an identifying numerical basis improves acquisition/transfer of an opaque boundary relation relative to an under-resolved slice, while separating predicate learning from action policy.

Even a strong positive result would authorize only a local construction law under this learner/transport regime. It would not redefine CFE as machine teaching.

## Anti-collapse rules

Do not silently redefine CFE as:
- grouping related examples;
- better curricula;
- better SFT ordering;
- efficient target-rule teaching;
- synthetic-data coverage;
- optimizer scheduling;
- prompt engineering;
- benchmark training.

Those can be mechanisms or experimental tools inside CFE.

They are not the root object.

## Research control question

Every major CFE branch should ask:

> **Are we learning how to engineer developmental experience, or have we accidentally reduced the problem to how to train this benchmark better?**

If the latter, label the work as a local experimental specialization and do not allow it to redefine the program.

## Change rule

Mechanisms, representations, field definitions, compilers, optimizer models, evaluation methods, and terminology may change when evidence requires it.

The program-level intent may change only if evidence shows that the intent itself is incoherent, impossible, or materially wrong.

Until then, local experimental failure changes the mechanism, not the Commander's Intent.
