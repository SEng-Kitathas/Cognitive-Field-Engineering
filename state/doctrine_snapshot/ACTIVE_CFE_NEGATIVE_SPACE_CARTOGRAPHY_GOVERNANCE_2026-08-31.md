## Coequal relationship with Rahl SOP
The active Rahl R3.1 SOP and CFE Cartography Governance are peers in operational priority but govern different questions.

- **Rahl SOP:** how work is controlled, typed, inspected, executed, verified, and reported.
- **CFE Cartography:** where the current experiment sits in the intervention space, what the existing evidence excludes/supports, which negative-space holes remain, and which next experiment most reduces uncertainty.

Neither may overwrite scientific evidence. Neither may silently override a frozen experiment contract.

If they appear to conflict:
1. explicit current operator instruction wins;
2. frozen scientific contracts / exact project obligations control their scope;
3. scientific evidence controls scientific truth;
4. Rahl governs process integrity;
5. Cartography governs experiment-space accounting and selection.

## Mandatory experiment lifecycle integration
### Before experiment selection
The active map SHALL be read and reconciled. Candidate experiments SHALL state:
- current occupied cell or parent cell;
- exact axis/axes being moved;
- which negative-space hole is being measured;
- what prior ambiguity that move is expected to reduce;
- whether the move is a main-effect/local-derivative probe or an interaction probe.

### Before preregistration
The candidate SHALL be checked against the map for accidental multi-axis movement. If more than one high-level axis changes, that fact must be explicit and justified.

### After execution
The map SHALL be updated only after evidence is admitted. Execution failures remain execution lineage and do not occupy a scientific cell.

### After disposition
The result SHALL update:
- occupied-cell status;
- boundary constraints;
- killed/weakened hypotheses;
- neighboring-hole ranking;
- whole-shape hypothesis confidence/uncertainty.

## Search strategy
Default strategy:
> **Map local derivatives around characterized cells before exploring distant combinations.**

Prefer:
1. one-axis moves adjacent to characterized cells;
2. holes adjacent to multiple characterized cells;
3. cells that discriminate between surviving explanations;
4. interactions only after component axes have local evidence.

Do not default to random raw-arrangement search.

`RAW CORPUS COMBINATORICS != GOVERNING MECHANISM SPACE`
`NEGATIVE RESULT = BOUNDARY INFORMATION`
`EXPERIMENT SELECTION = UNCERTAINTY REDUCTION OVER THE SHAPE`

## Per-turn obligation
When scientific state changes, cartography freshness is a no-stale completion requirement equal in priority to SOP/continuity freshness. The turn is incomplete if the active map still describes the prior scientific frontier.
