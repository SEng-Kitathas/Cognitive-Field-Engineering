# AI Reasoning Archaeology — Temporal/Causal LBE Program

Date: 2026-09-02
Status: **FIRST-CLASS HIGH-VALUE RESEARCH PROGRAM / ACTIVE BUILD**

## Commander-level objective
Build a longitudinal semantic field of reasoning artifacts across the history of AI, from early symbolic problem solvers through expert systems, cognitive architectures, search/RL systems, and modern language-model reasoning.

The goal is not a museum timeline and not a leaderboard of SOTA systems.

The goal is to recover the **million silent things** encoded by each reasoning artifact:
- what state had to exist;
- what distinctions were represented;
- what hypotheses were kept live;
- what was allowed to branch;
- what could be rolled back;
- what counted as evidence;
- what had authority to decide;
- what representation changes were possible;
- what order of implications mattered;
- what temporal history remained consequential;
- what was learned at train time vs searched at test time;
- what new capability appeared after which architectural/training pressure;
- what older reasoning structures later reappeared in new substrates.

Then reason **forward and backward** over that field:

`artifact history -> recurring structure -> candidate invariant -> temporal ordering -> competing causal explanations -> discriminating historical/experimental tests`

and:

`later capability -> required observable structure -> earliest prior embodiment -> missing interval -> candidate enabling change`

## Core laws

`FIRST PUBLIC APPEARANCE != FIRST EXISTENCE`

## Quarry policy
The quarry may include:
- public archival program traces;
- published proof/search examples;
- explanation-system transcripts;
- human-protocol / machine-protocol comparisons;
- open-source execution traces;
- published papers with algorithmic trace diagrams;
- open visible LLM reasoning traces;
- bounded published examples of proprietary reasoning/reconstructed reasoning where already lawfully public.

The quarry SHALL NOT:
- perform decryption/exploitation to recover private hidden reasoning;
- bulk ingest secret-bearing proprietary logs;
- redistribute copyrighted/proprietary trace corpora;
- store credentials/PII/private tool state;
- promote raw hidden reasoning into Standard Uplift training data merely because it was published or recovered.

For sensitive/proprietary specimens:

`PUBLIC SPECIMEN -> STRIP AT INGRESS -> STRUCTURAL ANNOTATION -> RAW TEXT DISCARDED/NOT PERSISTED`

Store only the minimum bounded evidence necessary to justify the structural inference.

## LBE translation
Each historical reasoning specimen becomes an evidence-bearing LBE composite rather than a flat document.

### Source layer
Exact artifact, date, author/system, archive/publication, rights class, trace surface, and acquisition method.

### Semantic entity layer
Entities may include:
- goals/subgoals;
- objects/world states;
- operators/actions;
- hypotheses;
- constraints;
- rules;
- differences/residuals;
- evidence observations;
- uncertainty states;
- representations/coordinate systems;
- branches;
- verifiers/reward signals;
- memory/chunks;
- tool calls/results;
- final claims.

### Typed relation layer
Candidate relations include:
- `DEPENDS_ON`
- `SUPPORTS`
- `CONTRADICTS`
- `REFINES`
- `SPECIALIZES`
- `MERGES_WITH`
- `SPLITS_FROM`
- `INVALIDATES`
- `ROLLS_BACK`
- `PRESERVES`
- `PREDICTS`
- `TESTS`
- `VERIFIES`
- `FALSIFIES`
- `CAUSES_OBSERVATION`
- `SELECTS_BRANCH`
- `PRUNES_BRANCH`
- `BACKS_UP_VALUE_TO`
- `UPDATES_CURRENTNESS_OF`
- `CHANGES_REPRESENTATION_OF`
- `LEARNED_FROM`
- `DISTILLED_FROM`
- `HISTORICALLY_PRECEDES`
- `POSSIBLE_LINEAGE_OF`
- `FUNCTIONAL_ISOMORPH_OF`

### State/control planes
Do not flatten these:
1. **Object state** — task/world facts and observations.
2. **Hypothesis state** — candidate solutions/mechanisms.
3. **Representation state** — available distinctions/coordinates.
4. **Control state** — search, branch, backtrack, act, verify, defer.
5. **Authority state** — what evidence/result is currently allowed to adjudicate.
6. **Learning state** — what is ephemeral test-time state vs persistent learned/chunked/weight-updated state.

### Evidence layer
Every inferred primitive/invariant carries:
- exact source pointer;
- observed vs inferred vs speculative status;
- competing interpretation;
- confidence;
- temporal currentness;
- rights/privacy handling;
- falsifier/counterexample target.

## Temporal-causal cartography
The field SHALL separate at least four edge classes:

### T1 — Chronology
`A HISTORICALLY_PRECEDES B`

No causal implication.

### T2 — Documented lineage
`B CITES / BUILDS_ON / DISTILLS_FROM A`

Requires explicit evidence.

### T3 — Functional recurrence
`A FUNCTIONAL_ISOMORPH_OF B`

Same high-level structural function under different implementation/substrate. This is a research inference, not lineage.

### T4 — Candidate causal enabling relation
`CHANGE X MAY HAVE ENABLED STRUCTURE Y`

Requires competing explanations and a discriminator. Never infer this from chronology alone.

## "Million silent things" annotation program
For each specimen ask:

### State
- What must already be active for this trace step to make sense?
- What is hard/current state versus scratch/candidate state?
- Which state survives branch failure?
- Which state becomes stale?

### Hypotheses
- Is more than one live possibility represented?
- Are alternatives explicit, sampled, implicit, or collapsed?
- What causes split/merge/prune?
- Are currently consequence-equivalent hypotheses compressed?

### Representation
- What distinctions can the system express?
- Are task-relevant states aliased?
- Can the representation refine or be replaced?
- Does failure trigger missing-distinction discovery?

### Control
- What triggers search, subgoal creation, backtracking, tool use, verification, or termination?
- Is control fixed, heuristic, learned, self-generated, or externally orchestrated?

### Authority
- What gets final say: rule, heuristic, evaluator, environment, proof checker, reward, majority vote, model self-evaluation, human label?
- Is verification orthogonal to generation?

### Time/history
- Which earlier events remain consequential?
- Does the system revisit earlier state?
- Does successful deliberation become persistent learning?
- Is path dependence observable?

### Compression
- What information disappears as reasoning becomes more compressed?
- Which relational/dependency structure survives?
- Does later performance rely on hidden learned compression of formerly explicit search?

### Failure
- Is failure object-level, hypothesis-level, representation-level, control-level, evaluator-level, or authority-level?
- Is rollback local or global?
- Are unknowns preserved?

## Reverse reasoning program
Once enough specimens are annotated, reason backward from later structures.

Example:

`modern representation refinement observed`
`-> find earliest trace with same functional transition`
`-> identify intervening systems where transition absent/externally supplied`
`-> identify architectural/training changes coincident with reappearance`
`-> enumerate alternative explanations`
`-> design modern matched tests where possible`

Do not infer historical causation from recurrence. Use recurrence to propose candidate invariants and missing discriminators.

## Initial comparative hypotheses

### H1 — Reasoning primitives recur before their modern names
Subgoal reduction, branch evaluation, explanation lineage, backtracking, verification, and learned shortcuts may recur across symbolic, search, and LLM substrates.

### H2 — Progress often moves structure between planes
A structure may move from explicit symbolic control to learned latent policy, then reappear as explicit language/tool orchestration.

### H3 — Multi-hypothesis competence depends on preserving consequence-distinct alternatives without materializing all possibilities
Historical search systems may provide useful external embodiments of this problem before LLMs.

### H4 — Representation repair is an old problem with new surfaces
Early systems often required designer-fixed representations. Later systems may gain limited ability to generate/refine representations during inference.

### H5 — Reality coupling repeatedly repairs pure internal search
Proof checkers, environment actions, expert-system evidence, game outcomes, tools, and code execution may be recurrent ways of restoring external authority.

### H6 — Learning from successful search compresses explicit reasoning into future control
Soar chunking, policy/value learning, STaR, process supervision, distillation, and RL reasoning may be functionally related instances at very different substrates.

`FUNCTIONAL RELATION != SHARED MECHANISM`
