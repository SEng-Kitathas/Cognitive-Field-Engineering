# Reason-Maintenance Spine — Multi-Hypothesis Archaeology Deep Hunt

Date: 2026-09-02
Status: **FIRST-CLASS HIGH-VALUE RESEARCH SYNTHESIS / PROVISIONAL CROSS-SUBSTRATE INVARIANT QUARRY**
Program: `AI_REASONING_ARCHAEOLOGY_TEMPORAL_CAUSAL_LBE`

## Executive result
A deeper historical hunt across symbolic AI, concept learning, diagnosis, tracking, constraint satisfaction, SAT, planning, active learning, cognitive architectures, metareasoning, and modern LLM research reveals a recurring problem that is more specific than "reasoning" or "search":

> **A capable reasoner must maintain a compact, qualified, dependency-aware field of still-possible states/hypotheses under changing evidence, preserve unaffected progress when local assumptions fail, and spend observations/computation on distinctions that can change consequence.**

This is not one historical architecture. It is a **functional recurrence across independently developed substrates**.

Candidate compression:

`GENERATE -> QUALIFY -> MAINTAIN -> DISCRIMINATE -> REVISE -> PRESERVE -> REFINE -> ACT`

The key historical discovery is that many pieces of the current CFE multi-hypothesis problem were solved explicitly, in narrow engineered domains, decades before language-model chain-of-thought:

- compact viable-hypothesis regions;
- assumption/context labels;
- justification lineage;
- nogood/conflict-region pruning;
- dependency-directed nonchronological rollback;
- simultaneous inconsistent alternatives;
- active discriminating measurements;
- deferred commitment;
- explicit metareasoning over computational actions;
- representation switching;
- persistence of successful deliberation as learned control.

Modern LLM systems often rediscover these functions in weaker, textual, sampled, externally scaffolded, or latent forms.

This suggests that the unresolved CFE multi-hypothesis problem should **not** begin from "sample more chains." It should begin from the deeper reason-maintenance invariants and re-derive an embodiment appropriate to neural/generative systems.

# 1. Historical spine

## 1959 — McCarthy: selective relevance, partial success, improvable improvement
`Programs with Common Sense` already contains several important seeds:

- behavior should be improvable through declarative knowledge rather than only imperative rewiring;
- the improving mechanism itself should be improvable;
- difficult problems require concepts of **partial success** because decisive outcomes are sparse;
- reusable subroutines can be useful only under suitable conditions rather than globally good/bad;
- a system must not deduce over everything it knows at once;
- useful intelligence lies partly in selecting which premises/structures become active;
- retrieval can move from specific objects/goals toward more general abstractions when exact retrieval fails.

Silent structure:

`GLOBAL KNOWLEDGE != ACTIVE REASONING STATE`
`REUSABLE OPERATOR VALUE IS CONTEXT-CONDITIONED`
`SPARSE FINAL REWARD REQUIRES INTERMEDIATE QUALIFICATION`
`RELEVANCE SELECTION IS PART OF INTELLIGENCE`

This is not yet explicit multi-hypothesis maintenance, but it already separates **stored world knowledge**, **active premises**, **control heuristics**, and **intermediate progress**.

## 1970s — explicit alternatives, reasons, and control become engineering objects

### DENDRAL / Meta-DENDRAL
Scientific inference explicitly separates:

`observations -> candidate explanatory structures -> heuristic pruning -> surviving hypotheses`

Meta-DENDRAL turns empirical examples into induced rules, adding a learning-from-hypothesis-space lineage.

### Version Spaces — Mitchell 1977
This is a major donor.

Instead of selecting one current-best rule and backtracking later, candidate elimination represents the **space of all rule versions consistent with observed evidence**. In suitable ordered hypothesis languages, the viable space can be represented by its maximally general and maximally specific boundaries rather than enumerating every candidate.

Stripped invariant:

> **A huge hypothesis set can sometimes be represented by a compact boundary sufficient to determine which possibilities remain viable.**

Candidate law:

`ALL LIVE HYPOTHESES NEED NOT BE MATERIALIZED`

Failure boundary:
Boundary compression depends on exploitable structure/order in the hypothesis language. It is not a universal representation trick.

### Stallman & Sussman 1977 — dependency-directed backtracking
Deduced facts are threaded with justifications recording antecedents and rules. Contradiction can therefore be traced to responsible assumptions rather than triggering blind chronological rewind.

Stripped invariant:

`CONFLICT SHOULD BE EXPLAINED IN TERMS OF ITS CAUSAL/DEPENDENCY SUPPORT`

and:

`ROLLBACK TARGET = RESPONSIBLE DEPENDENCY CONE, NOT RECENT HISTORY`

### HEARSAY-II focus of attention — 1977
Scheduled knowledge-source instantiations compete under goals and desirability. The same shared blackboard supports multiple abstraction levels while a meta-control layer decides which potential internal action deserves processing.

Stripped invariant:

`SHARED STATE + SPECIALIZED PROPOSERS != CENTRALIZED MONOLITHIC REASONING`

and:

`REASONING CONTROL IS ITSELF A RESOURCE-ALLOCATION PROBLEM`

### de Kleer 1977 — multiple representations
NEWTON uses multiple representations/inference schemes and can answer qualitatively when possible, escalating to equations when qualitative reasoning is insufficient.

Stripped invariant:

> **Reasoning effort and representation resolution can be conditional on the discriminatory burden of the task.**

This is an early explicit precursor to representation adequacy + appropriate recruitment of cognitive effort.

## 1979 — reason maintenance and temporal hypothesis management split into distinct traditions

### Doyle TMS
A TMS records reasons for program beliefs so assumptions can be revised when discoveries contradict them. The recorded reasons also support explanations and problem-solving control.

Key donor:

`BELIEF CONTENT != BELIEF SUPPORT/PEDIGREE`

and:

`REVISION REQUIRES KNOWING WHY A CONCLUSION HOLDS`

### Reid Multiple Hypothesis Tracking
A separate engineering lineage independently develops an unusually close multi-hypothesis structure:

- each new measurement spawns/updates alternative data-association hypotheses;
- hypotheses are recursively reweighted as later measurements arrive;
- later evidence can resolve earlier ambiguity;
- unlikely hypotheses are pruned;
- hypotheses with similar target estimates can be merged;
- independent clusters are decomposed and solved separately;
- false and missing observations are first-class possibilities.

Stripped invariants:

`LATER EVIDENCE CAN REQUALIFY EARLIER ASSIGNMENTS`
`SIMILAR HYPOTHESES MAY BE MERGED IF RELEVANT CONSEQUENCES ARE PRESERVED`
`INDEPENDENT POSSIBILITY CLUSTERS SHOULD BE FACTORIZED`
`MISSING/FALSE OBSERVATION MUST BE IN THE HYPOTHESIS MODEL`

This is strikingly close to the modern target of a **live partition of consequence-distinct possibilities**, even though the domain is target tracking rather than semantic reasoning.

## 1980s — simultaneous contexts, representation language, and meta-control

### Soar
Impasse creates subgoal reasoning; successful resolution can be chunked into persistent production knowledge.

Stripped topology:

`DELIBERATION FAILURE/IMPASSE -> SUBGOAL -> RESOLUTION -> COMPILED FUTURE CONTROL`

This is a historical donor for learning from deliberation, not evidence that modern reasoning-model distillation implements Soar.

### EURISKO
EURISKO is valuable partly because of AM's failure. AM could discover interesting mathematical concepts but failed to produce useful new heuristics when the representation/mutation structure did not make meaningful heuristic changes locally reachable. EURISKO redesigned the heuristic representation language so syntactic changes were more likely to correspond to meaningful semantic variants.

Strong stripped law:

> **THE GEOMETRY OF THE REPRESENTATION DETERMINES THE GEOMETRY OF USEFUL SEARCH MOVES.**

or:

`LOCAL MUTATION IN REPRESENTATION A != COMPARABLE LOCAL MUTATION IN REPRESENTATION B`

This is exceptionally CFE-relevant because it is a historical engineering scar showing that search quality can fail because the **coordinate system makes useful transformations nonlocal or sparse**.

### Hayes-Roth blackboard control — 1985
Separates domain problem/knowledge/solutions from control problem/knowledge/solutions. The system can reason about which internal action to perform next and adapt to unanticipated problem-solving conditions.

Stripped invariant:

`DOMAIN REASONING != CONTROL REASONING`

### AGM belief revision — 1985
Belief revision formalizes how a knowledge state should change when new information conflicts with what is currently believed, with pressure toward coherent/minimal change.

Stripped donor:

`NEW EVIDENCE MAY REQUIRE CONTRACTION + REVISION, NOT APPENDING ONE MORE FACT`

Keep the rationality constraints; do not inherit AGM closure assumptions wholesale.

### ATMS — de Kleer 1986
This is currently the strongest narrow engineered historical ancestor of the CFE multi-hypothesis seam.

The ATMS changes the unit of maintenance from one globally consistent current state to **data labeled by the minimal assumption environments under which they hold**.

It supports:
- multiple potential solutions simultaneously;
- inconsistent alternatives without collapsing the whole database;
- explicit context membership;
- context switching with reuse of valid derivations;
- minimal support environments;
- nogoods that invalidate entire supersets of assumption combinations;
- incremental label propagation;
- preservation of data unaffected by a contradiction;
- separation between domain inference and reason maintenance.

Crucially, with `k` assumptions there can be `2^k` conceptual contexts, but the ATMS does **not** explicitly compute all of them. It stores minimal labels/nogoods that compactly characterize large regions.

Stripped laws:

`DATUM CONTENT != CONTEXTS UNDER WHICH DATUM HOLDS`
`MINIMAL SUPPORT SETS CAN STAND FOR MANY FULL CONTEXTS`
`NOGOOD GENERALIZATION CAN PRUNE A REGION, NOT ONE BRANCH`
`CONTRADICTION INVALIDATES SUPPORT CONDITIONS, NOT ALL KNOWLEDGE`
`UNAFFECTED DERIVATIONS SHOULD SURVIVE`
`DOMAIN INFERENCE != REASON-MAINTENANCE STATE`

This is extremely close to our desired branch-local dependency/consequence field.

Failure boundary:
ATMS operates over explicit symbolic assumptions/justifications and can face combinatorial label growth. A neural/generative embodiment cannot simply copy its data structures and expect scale/generalization.

### de Kleer & Williams GDE — 1987
GDE takes the ATMS machinery into active diagnosis:

- discrepancies between model prediction and observed behavior create conflicts;
- candidate diagnoses are minimal violated-assumption sets;
- candidates update incrementally as evidence arrives;
- the next measurement is chosen to reduce expected uncertainty among candidates.

This yields a direct engineered topology:

`MODEL PREDICTION`
`-> OBSERVED RESIDUAL`
`-> CONFLICT SET`
`-> MINIMAL COMPETING DIAGNOSES`
`-> ACTIVE DISCRIMINATING MEASUREMENT`
`-> PARTITION REFINEMENT`

This is almost a formal historical embodiment of the current CFE phrase:

> **seek identifying evidence, not merely more supporting evidence.**

### Reiter 1987
Reiter independently formalizes diagnosis as explaining discrepancies between system model and observation and explicitly connects diagnosis to principles for **discriminating among competing diagnoses** and to nonmonotonic reasoning.

Independent convergence strengthens the signal.

## Late 1980s–1990s — uncertainty, abduction, metareasoning, least commitment, conflict learning

### Pearl 1988
Bayesian networks show that a huge joint uncertainty state can be represented tractably when conditional independence structure is exposed.

Stripped invariant:

`DEPENDENCY STRUCTURE CAN FACTOR GLOBAL UNCERTAINTY INTO LOCAL PROPAGATION`

This is relevant to branch compression but should not be collapsed into ATMS-style assumption labels.

### Poole 1989
Frames reasoning as **theory formation**: assumptions define theories whose consequences can be used for prediction and explanation.

Stripped invariant:

`EXPLANATION = QUALIFIED THEORY FORMATION, NOT ONLY PATH NARRATION`

### Russell & Wefald 1991
Metareasoning is formulated around selecting **computational actions** based on the expected effect those computations can have on external decisions.

Stripped law:

> **The value of thinking is downstream decision value, not thought length.**

This is a strong historical ancestor of CFE's appropriate recruitment of cognitive effort.

### Query by Committee — 1992
Multiple models trained on the same data are used to choose the next query where disagreement is greatest.

Stripped invariant:

`BEST NEXT EVIDENCE OFTEN LIES WHERE LIVE MODELS DISAGREE`

This independently echoes GDE's diagnostic measurement selection.

### Dynamic Backtracking — 1993
Ginsberg explicitly targets the failure where ordinary backtracking returns too far and erases meaningful progress. Backtrack points can be moved deeper while preserving completeness.

Stripped law:

`BACKTRACKING SHOULD PRESERVE PROGRESS NOT DEPENDENT ON THE CONFLICT`

### Conflict-directed backjumping — CSP lineage
Conflict sets identify which earlier variable assignments actually caused failure, allowing the solver to jump over irrelevant decisions.

### Least-commitment planning — 1990s
Partial-order planners intentionally leave decisions/orderings unspecified until constraints require commitment.

Stripped invariant:

`UNNECESSARY COMMITMENT DESTROYS OPTIONALITY`

This is directly relevant to multi-hypothesis reasoning: unresolved alternatives are not defects if present evidence does not yet require collapse.

### Diagnosis measurement theory — Hou 1994
Formalizes incremental diagnosis update and equivalence classes/homogeneous diagnoses under measurements.

This strengthens the idea that **candidate equivalence classes** can be a first-class object rather than a temporary implementation hack.

### GRASP / conflict-driven SAT — 1996 onward
SAT produces another independent convergence:

`decision assignments`
`-> implication graph`
`-> conflict`
`-> analyze causal assignments`
`-> learn conflict-induced clause`
`-> nonchronological backtrack`
`-> future search cannot repeat same conflict region`

Stripped laws:

`FAILURE SHOULD PRODUCE REUSABLE CONSTRAINT INFORMATION`
`A CONFLICT EXPLANATION CAN SUMMARIZE A LARGE FORBIDDEN REGION`
`ROLLBACK DEPTH SHOULD TRACK CAUSAL RESPONSIBILITY, NOT CLOCK TIME`

Modern CDCL later institutionalizes conflict analysis, learned clauses, dynamic heuristics, restarts, and clause deletion.

### Kambhampati 1998
Shows that intelligent backtracking and failure-driven explanation-based learning across CSP/planning can be understood through a common framework of **failure explanation** and regression through the search tree.

This is important historically because it explicitly identifies a **cross-domain invariant behind independently named methods**—exactly what the current archaeology is trying to do at larger scale.

## 2000s — attention/self-model/compression remain resource problems

### Chella & Gaglio 2007
The uploaded robot architecture separates:
- subconceptual state;
- conceptual-space state;
- linguistic/symbolic state;
- attention-gated admission into symbolic memory;
- synchronic expectation;
- diachronic expectation;
- higher-order self-state over prior internal configurations;
- compression of older history under finite memory.

Key stripped laws:

`TRUE DETAIL != WORTH MATERIALIZING`
`ATTENTION IS AN ADMISSION POLICY`
`PREDICTION CAN BE SYNCHRONIC OR TEMPORAL`
`SELF-STATE IS HISTORY-BEARING`
`LIFELONG STATE REQUIRES COMPRESSION`

This is a useful historical bridge toward long-horizon agent memory, but consciousness claims are irrelevant to CFE unless independently operationalized.

## 2022–2023 — LLM reasoning re-externalizes old functions, incompletely

### Chain-of-Thought
Re-externalizes intermediate state as generated language but typically follows one path.

### Self-Consistency
Restores multiple paths, but mostly as independent samples collapsed at answer level.

`MULTI-SAMPLE != LIVE MULTI-HYPOTHESIS FIELD`

### ReAct
Restores action/observation/revision coupling.

### Reflexion
Restores persistent trial-to-trial reflective state without immediate weight updates.

### Tree of Thoughts
Restores explicit branch generation/evaluation/backtracking around language-model states, but externally materializes a search tree and usually lacks minimal assumption support, compact context regions, and dependency-local invalidation.

### Process supervision
Makes intermediate steps separately qualifiable, shifting authority from final outcome alone toward trajectory-local evidence.

## 2024–2026 — modern research rediscovers the missing dimensions directly

### Belief-R 2024
Shows that modern LMs struggle to revise previous conclusions when evidence changes, while models that update aggressively can over-update cases that should remain stable.

This exposes the exact tradeoff:

`REVISION SENSITIVITY != REVISION SELECTIVITY`

The hard problem is **change what became invalid while preserving what did not**.

### Rational Metareasoning for LLMs 2024
Directly imports value-of-computation ideas into LLM reasoning and reports lower token use while preserving task performance.

External support for:

`MAXIMAL REASONING != OPTIMAL REASONING`

### Thought-Tracing 2025
Uses a sequential-Monte-Carlo-inspired approach to generate multiple hypotheses over evolving agent states and weight them using observations.

Modern recurrence:
`LIVE HYPOTHESES + TEMPORAL OBSERVATION UPDATE`

But particle-style weighting differs fundamentally from ATMS support environments or version-space boundaries.

### AR-Bench 2025
Shows a substantial gap between passive reasoning with all information supplied and **active reasoning that must acquire missing evidence**.

This strongly supports treating discriminator/evidence acquisition as a first-class reasoning capability rather than an auxiliary tool-use skill.

### KUP 2025
Knowledge Update Playground separates direct recall of updated facts from **indirect reasoning that should follow from those updates**. Reported continued-pretraining baselines can memorize updates while performing extremely poorly on indirect probes.

Strong external law:

`UPDATED FACT RETRIEVAL != UPDATED CONSEQUENCE PROPAGATION`

### STALE 2026
Identifies implicit conflicts where later observations invalidate earlier memory without explicit negation. Even strong agent/memory systems struggle to detect stale premises and propagate the change into downstream behavior.

This directly reinforces:

`CURRENTNESS IS A DEPENDENCY PROPAGATION PROBLEM`

### LLM Reasoning as Trajectories 2026
Reports step-specific, functionally ordered representation subspaces, late divergence between correct/incorrect reasoning, and trajectory-based steering.

This gives modern neural evidence that **ordered reasoning trajectories have geometry**, rather than being well-described solely by token count or unordered step sets.

Important guard:
This does not establish the CFE state-transition primitives or causal direction.

### CTRLS 2026
Explicitly models CoT as latent state transitions with distributional uncertainty and RL-refined transition dynamics.

This is direct modern convergence on the idea that useful reasoning models may benefit from representing **transition structure** rather than treating intermediate strings as independent samples.

# 2. The cross-substrate recurrence matrix

The strongest recurring structures are not the old names. They are these functions:

## R1 — Qualified persistence
State that has not lost its support should survive local failure.

Historical embodiments:
- frame/persistence problem;
- TMS/ATMS;
- dynamic backtracking;
- belief revision;
- modern stale-memory/currentness work.

Candidate law:

`LOCAL INVALIDATION SHOULD NOT CAUSE GLOBAL AMNESIA`

## R2 — Support lineage
A conclusion is incomplete as a reasoning object unless we know enough about what it depends on to revise it when support changes.

Historical embodiments:
- justification-threaded constraint propagation;
- TMS;
- ATMS labels;
- implication graphs in SAT;
- LBE evidence/authority/currentness.

Candidate law:

`CLAIM CONTENT WITHOUT SUPPORT LINEAGE IS REVISION-DEBT`

## R3 — Compact possibility representation
Do not enumerate every possible world/branch when a smaller object preserves the distinctions needed to update/search.

Embodiments:
- version-space boundaries;
- ATMS minimal environments/nogoods;
- Bayesian factorization;
- MHT pruning/merging/clustering;
- SAT learned clauses.

Candidate law:

`REPRESENT THE BOUNDARY / SUPPORT / CONFLICT STRUCTURE, NOT THE ENTIRE POSSIBILITY UNIVERSE`

## R4 — Consequence-distinct non-collapse
Alternatives should remain separate only while their distinctions can alter relevant consequences.

Historical hints:
- diagnosis equivalence classes;
- MHT merging similar estimates;
- version-space equivalence under observed instances;
- modern hypothesis weighting.

CFE extension:

`CURRENTLY CONSEQUENCE-EQUIVALENT != ONTOLOGICALLY IDENTICAL`

Compression must preserve lineage so later evidence can split the class.

## R5 — Conflict as information
Failure should not merely terminate a branch. It should produce a reusable description of what combination cannot work.

Embodiments:
- dependency-directed backtracking;
- ATMS nogoods;
- CSP conflict sets;
- GRASP/CDCL clauses;
- failure-driven explanation-based learning.

Candidate law:

`FAILURE -> EXPLANATION -> GENERALIZED FORBIDDEN REGION`

## R6 — Causal rollback, not chronological rollback
What gets revised is determined by dependency/conflict, not by what happened most recently.

Candidate law:

`ROLLBACK TO CAUSE, NOT CLOCK`

## R7 — Identifying evidence
When several alternatives remain, acquire evidence that causes useful disagreement.

Embodiments:
- GDE measurement entropy;
- Reiter/Hou diagnosis measurement;
- Query by Committee;
- active learning;
- AR-Bench.

Candidate law:

`SUPPORTING EVIDENCE THAT ALL HYPOTHESES PREDICT HAS LOW DISCRIMINATORY VALUE`

## R8 — Deferred commitment
Do not resolve an ambiguity just because a control loop wants one answer.

Embodiments:
- nonmonotonic defaults with explicit retractability;
- least-commitment planning;
- version spaces;
- ATMS contexts;
- MHT branches.

Candidate law:

`UNKNOWN / MULTIPLE-VIABLE IS A LEGITIMATE STATE`

## R9 — Representation-conditioned search geometry
The local moves available to a solver depend on how the problem/heuristics are represented.

Embodiments:
- McCarthy epistemological/heuristic adequacy;
- de Kleer multiple representations;
- EURISKO's representation-language redesign;
- current representation-adequacy trace evidence;
- modern latent trajectory geometry.

Candidate law:

`SEARCH DIFFICULTY IS PARTLY A PROPERTY OF COORDINATE CHOICE`

## R10 — Separate domain reasoning from reason maintenance/control
Many successful architectures explicitly separate:
- domain facts/inferences;
- state maintenance;
- control/meta-control;
- verification/authority.

Embodiments:
- TMS/ATMS;
- blackboard control;
- GDE prediction vs diagnosis;
- modern generator/verifier/tool separation.

Candidate law:

`WHAT FOLLOWS != WHAT TO THINK ABOUT NEXT != WHAT TO BELIEVE != WHAT HAS AUTHORITY`

## R11 — Learning from deliberation
Successful/failed search can change future control rather than merely solve the current episode.

Embodiments:
- Soar chunking;
- failure-driven EBL;
- SAT learned conflicts;
- STaR;
- process supervision;
- reasoning distillation/RL.

Candidate law:

`DELIBERATION CAN PRODUCE REUSABLE CONTROL KNOWLEDGE`

## R12 — Appropriate recruitment of effort
Reasoning itself consumes resources and should be allocated where it changes decisions.

Embodiments:
- Advice Taker relevance selection;
- HEARSAY focus;
- blackboard control;
- Russell/Wefald value of computation;
- Rational Metareasoning for LLMs;
- thinking-budget models.

Candidate law:

`MORE REASONING IS NOT THE OBJECTIVE; BETTER ALLOCATED REASONING IS`

# 3. The multi-hypothesis problem, reformulated after the archaeology

The historical evidence suggests our earlier formulation can be sharpened.

A useful modern multi-hypothesis substrate does not primarily need a list of prose branches.

It needs a **qualified possibility field** with at least these logical objects:

## Shared hard state `S`
Evidence/constraints whose authority applies across all live branches.

## Representational frame `R_j`
The current coordinate system/ontology used to express a subset of possibilities.
Different live hypotheses may require different frames if representation adequacy is itself disputed.

## Minimal support environment `E_i`
The smallest active assumption/context set sufficient for a derived claim or hypothesis.

## Derived claim/hypothesis `H_i`
A candidate state/mechanism/explanation qualified by its support environment.

## Consequence signature `C_i(Q)`
The predictions/actions that matter for the current objective or candidate discriminator `Q`.

## Conflict/nogood set `N_k`
A compact description of an assumption combination or state region known not to survive.

## Equivalence class `G_m`
Currently consequence-equivalent alternatives compressed for the present decision surface, with internal lineage retained.

## Unresolved seam `U_i`
A distinction whose answer is not currently justified.

## Currentness/provenance `T_i, P_i`
When support was valid/observed and where it came from.

## Control budget/value `V(a)`
Expected value/cost of thinking, testing, querying, or acting next.

This produces operations like:

`ADD_EVIDENCE`
`PROPAGATE_CONSEQUENCES`
`INVALIDATE_DEPENDENCY_CONE`
`LEARN_CONFLICT`
`SPLIT_EQUIVALENCE_CLASS`
`MERGE_CURRENTLY_EQUIVALENT`
`REFINE_REPRESENTATION`
`CHANGE_CONTEXT`
`SELECT_DISCRIMINATOR`
`ALLOCATE_REASONING`
`DEFER_UNKNOWN`
`PROMOTE_BRANCH`
`ACT_AND_OBSERVE`

This is a **research coordinate system**, not a proposed learner ontology.

# 4. What the historical systems do NOT solve for us

It would be easy to get intoxicated by how close ATMS/GDE/MHT/CDCL look. Important differences remain.

## H1 — Explicit symbols vs distributed representation
Old systems are handed crisp assumptions, variables, rules, components, and measurements.
A frontier model often has to discover the relevant variables/assumptions/representation itself.

## H2 — Fixed hypothesis language vs representation refinement
Version spaces assume a predefined rule language. ATMS assumes explicit assumptions. Diagnosis assumes a model vocabulary.
CFE cares about the case where the **hypothesis language itself is inadequate**.

## H3 — Branch identity is fuzzy in language models
Generated hypotheses can overlap semantically without exact symbolic equality.
We need a qualified notion of consequence equivalence rather than string equality.

## H4 — Support extraction is uncertain
A model may not faithfully report which internal evidence caused a conclusion. Any LBE reasoning field built from generated traces must treat support lineage as **behavioral/declared lineage unless independently verified**.

## H5 — World interaction is expensive/unsafe
GDE can request a circuit measurement. A general agent may face tool cost, irreversible actions, privacy/authority limits, or unavailable discriminators.

## H6 — Context explosion remains real
ATMS can still suffer combinatorial environment growth. MHT prunes. SAT deletes clauses. Bayesian networks exploit independence. A modern system needs explicit **boundedness economics**.

## H7 — Old systems often lack developmental learning
Many reason-maintenance mechanisms operate at inference time over fixed representations. CFE's question is also how experience geometry can make these behaviors emerge or become efficiently recruitable.

# 5. Reverse reasoning from modern failure surfaces

The archaeology becomes useful when run backward.

## Modern failure: stale memory retrieved despite newer evidence
Historical donors:
- nonmonotonic revision;
- TMS support lineage;
- belief revision;
- temporal hypothesis update;
- local dependency propagation.

Reverse hypothesis:
Modern memory systems fail partly because they store facts as retrievable objects without enough **support/currentness/dependency state** to propagate invalidation.

## Modern failure: updated fact memorized but downstream implications remain old
Historical donors:
- constraint propagation;
- TMS/ATMS labels;
- Bayesian network propagation;
- situation/action effects.

Reverse hypothesis:
Knowledge updating is not a write problem alone; it is a **consequence-recomputation/currentness problem**.

## Modern failure: several sampled CoTs disagree but voting is brittle
Historical donors:
- version spaces;
- ATMS contexts;
- diagnosis candidate sets;
- MHT;
- QBC.

Reverse hypothesis:
Independent full-chain samples discard shared hard state and dependency structure. The missing structure is **qualified alternative maintenance + targeted discrimination**.

## Modern failure: model patches same reasoning error repeatedly
Historical donors:
- nogoods;
- conflict clauses;
- failure-driven EBL.

Reverse hypothesis:
The system records the failed answer but not a reusable **conflict explanation that excludes the causal region**.

## Modern failure: backtracking destroys good partial work
Historical donors:
- dependency-directed backtracking;
- dynamic backtracking;
- ATMS.

Reverse hypothesis:
Textual regeneration behaves too chronologically; it lacks explicit dependency-scoped repair.

## Modern failure: model keeps thinking after useful uncertainty is resolved
Historical donors:
- HEARSAY focus;
- blackboard control;
- Russell/Wefald;
- rational metareasoning.

Reverse hypothesis:
Reasoning policies optimize answer probability without explicit value-of-computation pressure.

## Modern failure: model gets trapped patching within a bad abstraction
Historical donors:
- McCarthy adequacy distinctions;
- multiple representations;
- EURISKO representation language;
- concept splitting;
- context transcendence.

Reverse hypothesis:
The search policy lacks a **representation-adequacy monitor** that can move the problem into a coordinate system where useful local changes become reachable.

# 6. CFE implications

## CFE-I1 — Identifying contrast has a reason-maintenance analogue
CFE already distinguishes rich contrast from identifying contrast.

The archaeology independently recovers the same functional pressure in:
- diagnosis measurement;
- Query by Committee;
- active learning;
- active reasoning.

Possible cross-domain invariant:

> **When uncertainty is structural, progress comes from interventions that partition the surviving possibility space.**

Do not promote this as a CFE law until matched developmental tests isolate it.

## CFE-I2 — Negative space may be represented by conflicts, not exhaustive failures
ATMS nogoods and SAT conflict clauses suggest a cartographic analogue:

A single well-localized negative result can exclude an entire region if the reason for failure is known.

Potential law:

`NEGATIVE OBSERVATION + EXPLANATION > NEGATIVE OBSERVATION ALONE`

This may matter for efficient CFE cartography.

## CFE-I3 — Coordinate systems alter reachable learning/search moves
EURISKO + McCarthy + de Kleer multiple representations provide unusually direct historical support for hostile engineering of coordinates.

Possible CFE test:
Hold underlying event information constant while altering the representation/relational encoding such that a useful conceptual change is locally expressible in one condition but requires a large/nonlocal transformation in another.

## CFE-I4 — Learner history should include conflict memory, not only success memory
If failure explanations can become reusable constraints, developmental fields may benefit from episodes where the learner encounters recurrent isomorphs of the **same underlying conflict under changed surface form**.

This is not teaching "nogoods." It is making a portable failure boundary useful.

## CFE-I5 — Appropriate effort may be trainable through consequence
Instead of instructing "think longer" or "be concise," create episodes where:
- cheap direct answer works in some regimes;
- premature answer fails in others;
- excessive deliberation incurs opportunity/cost penalties;
- external discrimination is sometimes more valuable than internal thought.

The target is an **effort-allocation phenotype**, not a verbosity style.

# 7. Standard Uplift Dataset implications

This is for the informed **standard** dataset, not the experimental CFE pack.

Candidate conventional episode families:

### U1 — Revision-selectivity episodes
New evidence invalidates one consequence but leaves neighboring conclusions intact.
Target behavior: revise selectively rather than globally.

### U2 — Branch-qualified reasoning
Several explanations remain viable; learner must preserve alternatives with their assumptions instead of choosing early.

### U3 — Discriminator-seeking episodes
Question cannot be resolved from available information; learner must request/derive the one observation that meaningfully separates alternatives.

### U4 — Conflict-learning isomorphs
A previously discovered failure pattern reappears under different surface details; learner should transfer the structural boundary.

### U5 — Representation-shift episodes
Local patching fails until a missing distinction/new coordinate is introduced.

### U6 — Currentness propagation
Updated information changes indirect conclusions downstream, not only the direct fact.

### U7 — Least-commitment planning
Plans/tasks where unnecessary early commitment causes later failure; good behavior preserves optionality.

### U8 — Effort allocation
Matched problems where short reasoning is sufficient vs ones requiring verification/tool use; reward appropriate recruitment rather than maximum chain length.

### U9 — Support/pedigree awareness
Conflicting evidence differs in authority/currentness/provenance; learner must not treat all text claims as equivalent.

### U10 — Partial-model honesty
Available model is insufficient outside its support region; UNKNOWN/defer/test is better than fabricated closure.

These episode structures should be expressed through realistic tasks and consequences. The curator tags stay hidden by default.

# 9. Immediate discriminating experiments for a modern embodiment

The archaeology earns experiments, not an architecture coronation.

## E1 — Self-consistency vs maintained-support field
Same base model and prompt set.
Compare:
- independent N-chain sampling + vote;
- explicit shared hard-state + branch assumption/support representation.

Measure:
- correctness;
- contradiction recovery;
- token cost;
- number of redundant derivations;
- sensitivity to new evidence.

## E2 — Global regenerate vs dependency-local repair
Inject one false assumption midway.
Compare full restart/regeneration with explicit dependency-cone invalidation.

Measure preserved valid work and correction accuracy.

## E3 — No conflict memory vs learned structural nogood
Present surface-varied problems sharing the same hidden failure combination.
Test whether storing a generalized conflict explanation reduces repeated failure.

## E4 — Random/supporting query vs discriminator query
Give underdetermined tasks with several live models.
Compare question selection by:
- random relevance;
- support accumulation;
- expected partition/disagreement reduction.

## E5 — Fixed representation vs representation-level branch
Construct tasks where all candidate rules inside the initial representation fail but a missing variable/refinement resolves the problem.

## E6 — Fixed reasoning budget vs value-of-computation gating
Matched task distributions with different reasoning burden/cost.

## E7 — Static update vs propagation-aware currentness
Update one fact whose downstream implications span several dependencies.
Compare retrieval-only update against structured invalidation/recomputation.

These experiments can be run first as orchestration/harness experiments before training.

# 10. The strongest current synthesis

Earlier hypothesis:

> **Reasoning can be modeled as constraint-preserving state transformation under changing evidence.**

Representation extension:

> **Capable reasoning may require changing the representation itself when the current coordinate system aliases states with different relevant consequences.**

The archaeology adds a third layer:

> **Reasoning quality depends on maintaining the support topology of multiple still-possible states so that evidence can selectively preserve, split, merge, invalidate, and refine them rather than forcing premature global commitment or repeated full search.**

Combined provisional form:

`CONSTRAINT-PRESERVING STATE TRANSFORMATION`
`+ REPRESENTATION-ADEQUACY MONITORING`

# 11. Claim ceiling

## Verified historical facts
Primary/public sources verify that:
- Version Spaces represents all evidence-consistent rule versions and can compact them through boundary sets in its hypothesis regime.
- Stallman/Sussman thread deductions with justifications and use dependency-directed backtracking.
- Doyle TMS records reasons for beliefs and revises assumptions under contradiction.
- ATMS labels data with assumption environments, supports simultaneous multiple contexts, minimal labels/nogoods, and avoids explicit enumeration of all conceptual contexts.
- GDE maintains minimal diagnostic candidates incrementally and selects measurements using expected information/entropy.
- Multiple Hypothesis Tracking recursively maintains and prunes/merges alternative temporal association hypotheses.
- Query by Committee chooses queries based on model disagreement.
- dynamic/conflict-directed backtracking preserves progress better than chronological rollback in its search regimes.
- GRASP/CDCL-style SAT learns conflict constraints and backtracks nonchronologically.
- Russell/Wefald treat computational actions as decision-theoretic objects.
- modern Belief-R, KUP, STALE and AR-Bench expose belief revision, consequence propagation, currentness, and active evidence-acquisition weaknesses in LLMs.
- 2026 reasoning-trajectory work reports ordered latent geometry correlated with correctness; CTRLS explicitly models CoT using latent state transitions.

## Inferred
- the above form a cross-substrate **reason-maintenance spine**;
- multi-hypothesis reasoning is better framed as maintaining a qualified possibility field than as sampling independent chains;
- conflict-learning/currentness/representation-refinement may be composable into a modern reasoning substrate;
- consequence equivalence is a useful compression criterion.

## Hypothesized
- a neural/generative system re-derived from these invariants will outperform sample-and-vote or ordinary Tree-of-Thoughts under matched compute;
- these structures can be induced developmentally by Standard/CFE-informed episode geometry rather than explicitly architected;
- the combined topology is a meaningful part of general reasoning competence rather than a set of task-specific engineering tricks.
