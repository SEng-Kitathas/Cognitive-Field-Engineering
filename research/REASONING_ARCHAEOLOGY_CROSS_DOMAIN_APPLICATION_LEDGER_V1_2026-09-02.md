# Reasoning Archaeology — Cross-Domain Application Ledger v1

Date: 2026-09-02
Status: **ACTIVE HIGH-VALUE TRANSFER LEDGER / STRIP-FOR-PARTS ONLY**

## Purpose
Prevent the historical reasoning findings from being trapped inside "reasoning trace" research.

The governing transfer rule is the same correction previously applied to LHIT:

`STEAL INVARIANTS NOT ABSTRACTIONS`

and:

`DONOR DOMAIN != DESTINATION DOMAIN`

If an invariant survives across independent historical substrates, ask where else the same consequence structure matters. Re-derive it under the receiving domain's constraints.

Do not copy ATMS into code, MHT into memory, or CDCL into research literally. Transfer the invariant, not the machinery.

---

# Invariant transfer map

## 1. Support lineage
### Stripped invariant
A conclusion is revision-safe only if enough of its support/dependency lineage is available to know what should change when support changes.

`CLAIM CONTENT WITHOUT SUPPORT LINEAGE = REVISION DEBT`

### Code/debugging
- map failing behavior to assumptions, inputs, modules, commits, tests, and state dependencies;
- when one assumption fails, invalidate only dependent conclusions/patches;
- retain unaffected diagnostics and verified behavior;
- code-review claims should carry test/static/runtime evidence class.

### Research
- hypotheses carry source evidence, assumptions, derivation chain, and currentness;
- new paper/result should demote only conclusions that depend on the defeated premise;
- preserve unresolved alternatives instead of rewriting history around the newest result.

### Planning/agents
- plan steps carry preconditions and dependency links;
- changed resource/authorization/currentness invalidates downstream steps, not the whole plan;
- tool observations update the plan through the dependency graph.

### Memory/currentness
- stored facts carry temporal validity, provenance, and consequence dependencies;
- a new observation can invalidate downstream applicability without syntactic contradiction.

### Standard Uplift
Train episodes where one support premise changes and only its descendants should be revised.

---

## 2. Live consequence-distinct alternatives
### Stripped invariant
Do not collapse alternatives while available evidence leaves them consequence-distinct.

`MULTIPLE-VIABLE IS A LEGITIMATE STATE`

### Code/debugging
Maintain several root-cause hypotheses until tests distinguish them; do not commit to the first plausible bug story.

### Research
Maintain competing causal explanations with explicit predictions rather than blending them into vague uncertainty.

### Planning
Preserve optional routes/actions when current constraints do not require commitment.

### Tool use
If two API/data interpretations would imply different next actions, retain both until a tool result discriminates them.

### Standard Uplift
Include underdetermined tasks where the correct response is to preserve alternatives or ask for discriminating information.

---

## 3. Consequence-equivalence compression
### Stripped invariant
Alternatives that differ internally but predict the same currently relevant consequence may share storage/control, provided lineage is retained so later evidence can split them.

`CURRENTLY CONSEQUENCE-EQUIVALENT != ONTOLOGICALLY IDENTICAL`

### Code/debugging
Group root-cause hypotheses that imply the same next diagnostic test; split only after the test outcome differs.

### Research
Group theories by discriminating prediction rather than by wording; choose experiments across equivalence classes.

### Planning
Collapse plans that are operationally identical until a future constraint makes their distinction relevant.

### LBE
Represent equivalence relative to a query/objective, not global identity.

### Standard Uplift
Teach compact reasoning through tasks where several verbal explanations share one relevant prediction, then later evidence forces a split.

---

## 4. Rollback to cause, not clock
### Stripped invariant
Repair should target the dependency cause of conflict, not merely the most recent step.

### Code/debugging
A recent line may be innocent; trace failure to the causal configuration/state mutation.

### Research
A new result may invalidate an old assumption but leave newer downstream-independent findings intact.

### Planning
When a late step fails because of an early resource assumption, revise that assumption and its descendants rather than undoing every intervening valid choice.

### Conversation/LHIT
A correction to an earlier premise should update downstream conclusions selectively without deleting unrelated history.

### Standard Uplift
Construct tasks with deliberately nonchronological causal dependencies.

---

## 5. Failure -> reusable conflict information
### Stripped invariant
A failed episode is more valuable when it reveals a portable forbidden combination/boundary.

`FAILURE LABEL < FAILURE EXPLANATION < GENERALIZED FAILURE BOUNDARY`

### Code/debugging
Turn a regression into a test/invariant/static check that prevents the entire failure class.

### Research
Turn a falsified experiment into a recorded boundary on theory applicability, not just a negative result.

### Planning
Store why a route failed so later plans do not rediscover the same infeasible combination.

### Security/safety
Convert incidents into narrow, evidence-backed constraints rather than broad superstition.

### Standard Uplift
Use surface-varied isomorphs where transferring a prior failure boundary saves reasoning.

---

## 6. Active discriminator selection
### Stripped invariant
When uncertainty is structural, the next useful observation is often the one that partitions surviving alternatives, not the one that merely adds support to all of them.

### Code/debugging
Choose the next test/log/probe that most separates root-cause hypotheses.

### Research
Choose experiments for competing predictions / information gain rather than publication convenience.

### Planning/tools
Ask the one question/API query that changes which action is appropriate.

### Memory
If state is ambiguous, request current information rather than guessing from stale context.

### Standard Uplift
Include tasks where information acquisition is part of the answer and irrelevant-but-plausible questions are available distractors.

---

## 7. Representation adequacy / missing distinction
### Stripped invariant
If local patches cannot reconcile consequence differences, the coordinate system may be aliasing states that need to be distinguished.

### Code/debugging
The bug may be in the data model/state representation, not a local conditional; add the missing state dimension rather than patching every symptom.

### Research
Persistent residual/sign flip may imply a missing variable/axis or wrong abstraction.

### Planning
A plan representation that omits authority, resource ownership, time windows, or uncertainty may make correct decisions unreachable.

### Memory
"User preference" may be too coarse if current state, temporary constraint, and durable preference require different representations.

### Standard Uplift
Use coarse-success -> boundary failure -> local patch failure -> finer distinction -> transfer episodes.

---

## 8. External/orthogonal authority
### Stripped invariant
Internal reasoning should yield to a stronger independent evidence channel where one exists.

### Code
Tests/runtime/static analysis beat confidence in a patch narrative.

### Research
Measurement/data beat desired theory; provenance and experimental controls matter.

### Planning/tools
Actual resource/status/readback beats assumed state.

### Math
Proof checker/brute force/reference calculation can adjudicate disputed derivation.

### Standard Uplift
Reward mode switches from internal reasoning to tool/check/measurement when appropriate.

---

## 9. Currentness propagation
### Stripped invariant
New information can change the applicability of old state indirectly through dependencies.

### Code
Configuration/schema/API changes can stale assumptions in downstream modules without directly editing them.

### Research
New calibration/methodology result can dequalify old conclusions built on an affected measurement chain.

### Planning
Resource/authority/time changes invalidate dependent actions.

### Memory
Location, injury, role, deadline, account state, or relationship context can stale prior advice without explicit negation.

### Standard Uplift
Include Type-II propagated-conflict episodes across many domains, not only conversational memory.

---

## 10. Learning from deliberation without copying the whole trace
### Stripped invariant
The durable object should be the useful dependency/strategy/conflict structure extracted from the episode, not every token/event.

### Code
Commit regression test + invariant + minimal fix rationale, not hours of debugging transcript.

### Research
Preserve discriminator, failure boundary, method scar, and evidence lineage rather than narrative meandering.

### Agents
Persist compact state/action lessons with conditions of applicability rather than whole conversation replay.

### Training data
Distill bounded state-changing reasoning events; reject performative/redundant self-talk.

### LBE
Materialize a composite whose boundary states requirements, provided capability, effects, hazards, currentness, and lineage.

---

## 11. Appropriate recruitment of reasoning effort
### Stripped invariant
Reasoning is a costly action whose value depends on how much it can change the decision/qualification state.

### Code
Do not deep-debug a component before cheap discriminating checks rule out configuration/input mistakes.

### Research
Do not run expensive experiments before lower-cost discriminators eliminate major hypothesis classes.

### Agents
Use cheap retrieval/direct response when sufficient; escalate to search/tool/code/checks when unresolved burden warrants it.

### Standard Uplift
Mix matched tasks where short reasoning is sufficient with tasks where verification or deeper search is necessary; penalize both premature closure and gratuitous rumination.

---

## 12. Consequential history, not context length
### Stripped invariant
History matters when earlier state changes later reachability, interpretation, support, or consequence.

### Code
Prior migration/commit/config changes matter if they alter current state dependencies.

### Research
Earlier experimental choices/calibrations matter if they condition later evidence.

### Planning
Past actions matter when they consume resources, create commitments, or change options.

### LHIT / conversation
Retain state because later behavior depends on it, not because it is old conversation text.

### Standard Uplift
Whole episodes should preserve the causal history required by later turns; otherwise the example is not LHIT-quality.

# Domain synthesis

## Code and debugging
The historical reason-maintenance package suggests a stronger coding phenotype than "write correct code":

`maintain candidate causes -> inspect dependency/state -> choose discriminating test -> localize conflict -> preserve valid work -> change representation if needed -> verify externally -> compile failure boundary into regression protection`

This is exactly how the reasoning archaeology should influence code training.

## Research
The target phenotype is not merely "cite papers":

`preserve competing models -> track evidence/pedigree/currentness -> distinguish observation/inference -> seek identifying experiment -> update locally -> retain UNKNOWN -> reformulate coordinate system when residuals persist`

## Planning / agent work
The target is:

`shared current state -> alternative plans -> least commitment -> resource/authority/currentness checks -> tool observation -> dependency-aware replan -> readback`

This strongly matches PCMMAD execution doctrine for non-accidental reasons, but historical recurrence does not make PCMMAD a universal architecture.

## Memory / long horizon
The target is latent/current state maintenance, not transcript retrieval:

`old supported state -> new observation -> direct/indirect dependency update -> stale-state invalidation -> preserve unrelated memory -> act on current state`

## Scientific/diagnostic reasoning
GDE/MHT/QBC-like invariants suggest:

`model/world residual -> live explanations -> discriminatory measurement -> update -> representation audit if residual persists`

## LBE
LBE should be able to represent the above as semantic state rather than prose:
- support environments;
- branch-local assumptions;
- conflict regions;
- currentness intervals;
- discriminator queries;
- consequence-equivalence classes;
- representation frames;
- authority transitions;
- rollback/invalidation edges;
- learned scars/failure boundaries.

## Standard Uplift
The informed standard corpus should expose these structures across domains so the learner sees many isomorphic opportunities to recruit them.

This is **not** the CFE experimental pack. CFE later tests whether specific placement/contrast/revisit geometry has causal developmental effects under matched controls.

# Transfer guard
A historical invariant is admitted to another domain only when:
1. the receiving domain has an analogous consequence/failure surface;
2. the donor mechanism is stripped to the smallest functional requirement;
3. authority/provenance/currentness are re-derived locally;
4. counterexamples are sought;
5. labels/ontology are not taught merely because the curator finds the analogy elegant.

`ISOMORPHISM IS A HYPOTHESIS UNTIL THE CONSEQUENCE STRUCTURE MATCHES`
