# CFE — Prerequisite Topology and Developmental Basin Hypothesis

Status: **HIGH-VALUE RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS / NOT SCIENTIFICALLY EARNED YET**
Date: 2026-09-02 22:10 Eastern Daylight Time

## Operator insight
The project may have overfocused on tracing fully formed adult-level task atoms into a field. A more fundamental developmental variable may be whether experiences arrive in an order that respects the logical/representational dependencies required to interpret later concepts.

The useful analogy is not literal school grade labels. It is a dependency partial order: primitive distinctions -> relations -> compositions -> abstractions -> transfer/revision.

## Candidate mechanism
`SAME FINAL INFORMATION != SAME DEVELOPMENTAL REPRESENTATION`

If concept/skill B depends on A for its most economical/appropriate representation, exposing B before A may force a workaround representation or basin. Later learning A may then be assimilated around the workaround rather than automatically rebuilding B from the intended primitive.

Candidate phenomenon: **developmental hysteresis / basin capture by premature abstractions**.

## Stronger candidate law
`COHERENT FIELD != DEVELOPMENTALLY VALID FIELD`

A field can be locally coherent and still be developmentally premature if the learner lacks the prerequisite distinctions needed to interpret it in the intended relation space.

## Relation to earned CFE results
- DD1 field-resolution support is compatible with the idea that identifying distinctions need to be jointly available when a relation is being formed.
- V14R2 horizon insufficiency is compatible with the idea that repeating dependent material cannot substitute for missing prerequisite structure.
- V10 relation-family/sign differences remain important and prevent reducing all CFE to prerequisite order.

These are compatibility arguments, not proof.

## Proposed new cartographic coordinate
`PREREQUISITE_TOPOLOGY / DEPENDENCY_ORDER`

This should be treated as distinct from generic temporal spacing. It asks whether the order of exposure respects a known dependency DAG/partial order.

## Clean experiment sketch
Construct a synthetic competence with an explicit dependency graph, e.g. `A -> B -> C -> D`. Hold atomic experiences, token count, dose, learner, evaluation, local support and co-visibility as closely matched as possible. Compare:

- **DEPENDENCY_RESPECTING:** prerequisite relations become available before dependent abstractions.
- **DEPENDENCY_VIOLATING:** same atoms/dose, but dependent abstractions arrive before their identifying prerequisites.

Measure primitive competence, composition, transfer, later relearning cost, representation-path sensitivity, and whether later prerequisite exposure repairs or preserves the early phenotype.

## Key discriminator
The important result is not merely final accuracy. Test whether equal final information produces different transfer/composition/revision behavior after different developmental orders.

## Guards
- `PREREQUISITE ORDER != EASY-TO-HARD CURRICULUM`.
- `LOGICAL DEPENDENCY != CURATOR ONTOLOGY BY DEFAULT`; dependency must be externally justified by task consequence/identifiability.
- `SCHOOL-GRADE ANALOGY != REQUIRED STAGE COUNT`.

ISD may ultimately benefit from developmental staging based on dependency structure, but no literal elementary/middle/high/college partition is frozen. First identify dependency layers and test whether staged ordering matters causally.
