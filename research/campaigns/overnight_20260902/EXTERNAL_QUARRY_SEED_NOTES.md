- Energy-based continual learning: changing the training objective can reduce interference; reminder that apparent field effects may be optimizer/objective-mediated rather than purely representational.
- Generalized synchronization in reservoir computing: pre-training dynamical diagnostics can predict usable reservoir regimes; possible donor pattern for pre-learning field qualification.
