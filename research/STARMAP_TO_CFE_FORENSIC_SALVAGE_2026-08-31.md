## Bottom line
StarMap is a real architectural lineage, not a stray label. Across the recovered corpus it evolves from a radial relevance map into an executable cognitive-geometry graph, then into topology-aware memory/search and finally into a Pattern Starmap whose declarative pattern source generates enforcement behavior.

The strongest reusable idea for CFE is **not** the old embedding geometry. It is the separation and later reunification of:

`authoritative relational field specification -> generated operational projections`

For CFE that becomes:

`FIELD SPEC -> EXPOSURE COMPILER + EVALUATION COMPILER + TRACE COMPILER + FIELD DIAGNOSTICS`

The learner sees only the compiled developmental experiences. The curator-side field graph remains instrumentation unless an experiment explicitly exposes some of its structure.

## Historical lineage stripped for parts
### 1. Radial relevance / degree of removal
Early StarMap placed nearby concepts closer to a core and distant relations farther away. **Keep:** multiscale locality and degree-of-removal as analysis concepts. **Discard:** literal radial layout as ontology.

### 2. Executable StarMap Geometry Engine
The v4.1 engine represented nodes with salience, novelty and order; edges with weight, angle, tension and type; clusters with centroids/coherence; and persistence into CIL history. **Keep:** typed nodes/edges, history-sensitive novelty, persistent identity. **Discard:** word extraction, cosine-threshold relation discovery, k-means-as-neighborhood, salience-difference-as-tension.

### 3. Domain-vector geometry
A later non-executable spec represented domain stance x evidence x relevance and measured cross-domain angular coherence. **Keep:** explicit separation of stance/evidence/relevance and conflict surfacing. **Discard:** vector-angle agreement as evidence that domains or developmental relations are actually coherent.

### 4. NEAL topology evolution
Later NEAL made StarMap a topology-aware graph over persistent storage with topology addresses, hot/cold edges, spreading activation, graph search and rehydration. **Keep:** stable topology identity, local/long-range edge distinction, graph reach diagnostics, bounded propagation, provenance. **Quarantine:** PCA/Hilbert physical layout, hardware locality, entropy-triggered physical reordering as implementation/control mechanisms rather than CFE laws.

### 5. Pattern Starmap / Forge
The Forge lineage reached the most important abstraction: the pattern genome should not coexist beside hard-coded gates; the genome should generate detection, remediation, explanation and evidence logic. **Promote strongly to CFE tooling:** the field definition should generate the operational surfaces that enact and test it, preventing curator schema and compiler behavior from drifting apart.

## CFE-native field schema candidate
A curator-side CFE field node should be able to carry stable identity plus optional observed properties such as source/provenance, event time, exposure time, consequence time, uncertainty state, staleness, recurrence count and developmental episode.

Edges should be typed, directional when appropriate, and multi-relational. Candidate types:
- `TEMPORAL_PRECEDES` / `TEMPORAL_REVISITS`
- `CAUSES` / `ENABLES` / `PREVENTS`
- `ATTRIBUTED_TO` / `DERIVED_FROM`
- `CONTRADICTS` / `SUPPORTS`
- `CONTRASTS_WITH` / `ANTI_ISOMORPH_OF`
- `SAME_LATENT_RELATION`
- `CONSEQUENCE_OF`
- `STALE_RELATIVE_TO`
- `RECURS_WITH`
- `LONG_RANGE_BRIDGE`

These labels belong to the **curator/control plane**. They are not learner ontology.

## Historical isomorphism
The operator's older StarMap work repeatedly treated **placement, neighborhood, distance, traversal, persistence and provenance** as functional parts of a cognitive system. CFE independently arrived at an experimentally disciplined version of a related intuition: learner-visible and optimizer-visible arrangement changes phenotype.

That is a real lineage connection. It is not yet evidence that the old StarMap mechanisms were correct. The correct move is exactly what was requested: strip the lineage for mechanisms, keep the useful abstractions, and make every imported part earn its place under CFE experiments.
